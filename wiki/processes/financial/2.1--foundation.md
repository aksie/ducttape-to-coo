# Cash Flow Management — Foundation

> At 1–5 people, cash discipline is a weekly look at the bank — not a forecast model. Know the balance, the burn, and how many months you have left.

## What good looks like at this stage

- **Bank balance checked weekly** — not only when something feels tight.
- **You know your burn rate** (typical monthly cash out, or average of the last few months).
- **Runway is visible** in a simple spreadsheet, tied to the same numbers as the business plan / pitch deck.

## What you actually need to do

1. **Open the bank (or export) once a week** and write the balance in one sheet.
2. **Compute burn** = cash out last month (or a 3-month average) — one number founders share.
3. **Runway = cash ÷ burn** — keep that figure next to the plan numbers you show investors or yourselves.

## Warning signs you're behind

### Output quality
- **Nobody can say runway in months without opening three apps.**

### Founder time
- **Cash surprises** — a big bill or payroll hits and the balance was never looked at that week.

### Process entry
- **Pitch-deck runway and the bank sheet disagree** and nobody reconciles them.

## How this evolves next

At **First Hires**: monthly cash forecast 3–6 months out, alert below ~6 months runway, and payroll timing — see [2.1 at First Hires](2.1--first-hires.md).

## Tools & resources

- One spreadsheet (or your bank’s export + a single tab) — enough at this stage; forecasting tools wait until first hires.
