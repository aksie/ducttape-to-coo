# Accounts Receivable (Outgoing Invoices) — Early Revenue

> [First hires](2.3--first-hires.md) was invoice → track → chase by hand. Early revenue standardises templates, terms, and reminders — and you watch days-to-collect weekly.

## What good looks like at this stage

- **Every invoice uses the same template and payment terms** (30 days net is common in NL/EU B2B).
  <!-- sources: processes.json 2.3 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

- **Automated reminders fire before and after due date** — chasing is still personal for big accounts, but the system nudges by default.
  <!-- sources: processes.json 2.3 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

- **You know average days to collect (DSO) and review it weekly.**
  <!-- sources: processes.json 2.3 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

## What you actually need to do

1. **Lock one invoice template** in your accounting tool — line items, VAT, payment instructions, terms — and stop one-off formats.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 2.3 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

2. **Turn on automated payment reminders** (day 0, day 7 overdue, day 14) — keep [first-hires personal chase](2.3--first-hires.md) for strategic customers.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 2.3 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

3. **Add a DSO line to your weekly finance check:** total open AR ÷ average daily revenue (or simple days outstanding on open invoices).
   <!-- sources: processes.json 2.3 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

4. **Document standard terms in customer contracts** so invoice terms match what was sold.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 2.3 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

## Warning signs you're behind

### Output quality

- **Every customer has different payment terms buried in email** — invoices don't match contracts.
  <!-- sources: processes.json 2.3 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

### Founder time

- **DSO creeping up but nobody tracks it until cash is tight.**
  <!-- sources: processes.json 2.3 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

### Process entry

- **Reminders exist but overdue accounts still require a founder to notice.**
  <!-- sources: processes.json 2.3 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

## How this evolves next

At **growth**: milestone-triggered invoicing, escalation paths for overdue accounts, revenue recognition aligned to contract structure — see [2.3 at Growth](2.3--growth.md).
