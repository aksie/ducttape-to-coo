# Cash Flow Management — Growth

> Cash planning that starts when payroll is at risk is already late. This holds however the company is funded.

## What good looks like at this stage

Cash planning is a habit before it becomes an emergency.

## Warning signs you're behind

### Founder / key-person time

- **Cash planning sits informally with one founder and becomes real only when payroll is at risk.** When getting or using a credit facility is the first serious conversation, then you are too late.
