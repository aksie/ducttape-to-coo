# Accounts Payable & Expense Management — Early Revenue

> Volume and headcount at early revenue break informal [first-hires](2.4--first-hires.md) habits — you need named approvers, corporate cards, and cost-centre coding before books become archaeology.

## What good looks like at this stage

- **Approval thresholds are written:** who can spend what without sign-off, and who approves above each tier.
  <!-- sources: src-008 (founderpath), processes.json 2.4 stageFocus seed -->

- **Regular spenders have company cards** — not shared founder cards with end-of-month surprises.
  <!-- sources: src-018 (spendesk), processes.json 2.4 stageFocus seed -->

- **Vendor invoices are coded to a cost centre before payment** — books ready for budget variance and diligence.
  <!-- sources: processes.json 2.4 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

## What you actually need to do

1. **Publish a one-page approval matrix** — e.g. team lead to €500, founder to €5k, board above — and follow it on every AP run.
   <!-- sources: src-008 (founderpath) -->

2. **Issue corporate cards (or dedicated virtual cards) to roles that spend weekly** — travel, ads, tools — with per-card limits.
   <!-- sources: src-018 (spendesk), human:aksie | flags: unverified -->

3. **Require cost-centre (or category) on every invoice before your bookkeeper pays** — no "misc" bucket by default.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 2.4 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

4. **Keep monthly card reconciliation** from first hires — match receipt, category, approver before close.
   <!-- sources: src-009 (bench), src-008 (founderpath) -->

## Warning signs you're behind

### Output quality

- **P&L by category is useless because half of spend is uncoded.**
  <!-- sources: processes.json 2.4 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

### Founder time

- **Founders still approve every coffee receipt** because thresholds were never defined.
  <!-- sources: src-008 (founderpath) -->

### Process entry

- **New hires don't know how to spend or get reimbursed** — policy exists only verbally.
  <!-- sources: src-008 (founderpath), human:aksie | flags: unverified -->

## How this evolves next

At **growth**: delegated approvers, expense software, and audit-ready AP workflows — volume from [first-hires evolution note](2.4--first-hires.md).
