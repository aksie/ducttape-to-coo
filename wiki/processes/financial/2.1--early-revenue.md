# Cash Flow Management — Early Revenue

> Early revenue means enough moving parts that weekly bank checks aren't enough — you need a rolling forecast, budget variance, and scenarios before you hire or spend.

## What good looks like at this stage

- **A rolling 12-month cash forecast exists and is updated at least monthly.**
  Builds on [foundation](2.1--foundation.md) runway math; adds revenue assumptions and planned hires.
  <!-- sources: processes.json 2.1 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

- **Actuals are tracked against budget monthly** — founders see variance, not surprises at quarter-end.
  <!-- sources: processes.json 2.1 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

## What you actually need to do

1. **Maintain one cash forecast sheet:** opening balance, monthly inflows (revenue + funding), outflows (payroll, vendors, tax), closing balance — roll forward twelve months.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 2.1 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

2. **Reconcile forecast to bank monthly** — update actuals, revise assumptions when revenue or burn shifts.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 2.1 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

3. **Before any hire or major spend, run two scenarios:** base case and "what if this slips 3 months" — decide with numbers, not gut.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 2.1 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

4. **If runway drops below your board/investor threshold (~6–9 months), align [1.2b reporting](1.2b--early-revenue.md) and fundraising timeline** — don't wait for a crisis.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 2.1 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

## Warning signs you're behind

### Output quality

- **Forecast and bank balance diverge every month and nobody adjusts the model.**
  <!-- sources: processes.json 2.1 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

### Founder time

- **Hiring decisions made without updating runway** — "we'll figure out cash later."
  <!-- sources: processes.json 2.1 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

### Process entry

- **Only one founder can explain the forecast** — no shared sheet, no finance/admin backup.
  <!-- sources: processes.json 2.1 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

## How this evolves next

At **growth**: integrated planning with [2.2](2.2--growth.md), tighter board metrics, and formal budget cycles.
