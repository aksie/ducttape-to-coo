# Sales Operations — Early Revenue

> [First hires](5.1--first-hires.md) was a deal log and weekly founder review. Early revenue needs defined stages, CRM discipline, forecast, and comp everyone understands.

## What good looks like at this stage

- **Sales stages and exit criteria are written** — everyone uses the same definitions in the CRM.
  <!-- sources: processes.json 5.1 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

- **The CRM is the source of truth** — not parallel spreadsheets or founder memory.
  <!-- sources: processes.json 5.1 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

- **Simple pipeline forecast and documented quota/comp** — reps know how they're measured.
  <!-- sources: processes.json 5.1 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

## What you actually need to do

1. **Define 4–6 pipeline stages with exit criteria** (e.g. qualified, demo, proposal, verbal, closed) and enforce them in weekly pipeline review.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 5.1 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

2. **Rule: if it's not in the CRM, it doesn't exist** — migrate off side trackers; founders model the behaviour.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 5.1 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

3. **Build a monthly forecast from weighted pipeline** — stage × probability × amount — reviewed with leadership.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 5.1 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

4. **Document quota and commission (or OTE) in writing** — walk through with each rep; no verbal-only comp plans.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 5.1 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

## Warning signs you're behind

### Output quality

- **Two reps describe the same deal at different stages.**
  <!-- sources: processes.json 5.1 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

### Founder time

- **Forecast is rebuilt in slides every board meeting from scratch.**
  <!-- sources: processes.json 5.1 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

### Process entry

- **New rep joins and nobody explains stages or comp until first commission dispute.**
  <!-- sources: processes.json 5.1 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

At **growth**: dedicated sales ops, enforced hygiene, weekly pipeline ops — see [5.1 at Growth](5.1--growth.md).
