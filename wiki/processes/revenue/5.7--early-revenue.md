# Customer Data & Analytics — Early Revenue

> Clean CRM from [first hires](5.7--first-hires.md) becomes shared metrics, owners, and one dashboard — sales, CS, and finance align on MRR, churn, and NPS.

## What good looks like at this stage

- **Core customer metrics are named with a single owner each** — MRR, churn, NPS, pipeline, etc.
  <!-- sources: processes.json 5.7 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

- **One simple dashboard shows the same numbers leadership, sales, CS, and finance use.**
  <!-- sources: processes.json 5.7 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

## What you actually need to do

1. **List 5–8 core metrics** — define formula and data source for each; assign one DRI per metric.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 5.7 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

2. **Build a lightweight dashboard** (ChartMogul, Looker Studio, Metabase, or CRM reports) for MRR, churn, and NPS minimum.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 5.7 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

3. **Review the dashboard in weekly leadership** — disagreements on numbers mean definitions aren't shared yet.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 5.7 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

4. **Reconcile CRM revenue with finance invoices monthly** — catch classification drift early.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 5.7 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

## Warning signs you're behind

### Output quality

- **Board deck MRR ≠ finance MRR ≠ sales pipeline report.**
  <!-- sources: processes.json 5.7 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

### Founder time

- **Metrics pulled manually for every investor update.**
  <!-- sources: processes.json 5.7 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

### Process entry

- **New hire asks "where is the dashboard?" and there isn't one.**
  <!-- sources: processes.json 5.7 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->
