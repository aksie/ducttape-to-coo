# Service Delivery — Early Revenue

> Founder-led delivery from [first hires](5.6--first-hires.md) needs templates, cadence, scope control, capacity limits, and post-project learning.

## What good looks like at this stage

- **Kickoff, status, and scope-change steps are templated** — same structure every engagement.
  <!-- sources: processes.json 5.6 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

- **Capacity is visible** — you know how many concurrent projects the team can run without slippage.
  <!-- sources: processes.json 5.6 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

- **Post-delivery review captures what worked and what didn't.**
  <!-- sources: processes.json 5.6 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

## What you actually need to do

1. **Create kickoff template** — goals, timeline, roles, success criteria, comms cadence — used on every project start.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 5.6 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

2. **Set status update rhythm** (weekly email or call) with a one-page format — RAG status, blockers, next week.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 5.6 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

3. **Define scope-change process** — written change request, client sign-off, timeline/price impact — before extra work starts.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 5.6 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

4. **Track utilization or concurrent projects; say no when at capacity** — over-commitment shows up as churn and margin loss.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 5.6 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

5. **Run a 30-minute retro within two weeks of delivery close** — feed lessons into the next kickoff template.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 5.6 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

## Warning signs you're behind

### Output quality

- **Every project runs differently; clients get inconsistent experience.**
  <!-- sources: processes.json 5.6 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

### Founder time

- **Scope creep absorbed silently until margin disappears.**
  <!-- sources: processes.json 5.6 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

### Process entry

- **Sales promises dates delivery team hasn't seen.**
  <!-- sources: processes.json 5.6 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->
