# Customer Onboarding & Activation — Early Revenue

> White-glove onboarding for the first ten customers doesn't scale. Document steps, define "activated," and measure time-to-activation — building on [first hires](5.3--first-hires.md) personal delivery.

## What good looks like at this stage

- **Customer onboarding steps are written and repeatable** — not heroics by one founder or CS lead.
  <!-- sources: processes.json 5.3 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

- **"Activated" is defined** — the observable moment the customer gets first value (first login, first report, first transaction, etc.).
  <!-- sources: processes.json 5.3 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

- **Time-to-activation is tracked and reviewed monthly.**
  <!-- sources: processes.json 5.3 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

## What you actually need to do

1. **Document the onboarding checklist** — kickoff call, setup tasks, training, go-live — from what you did for early reference customers.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 5.3 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

2. **Define activation metric with product and CS** — one event or milestone per segment; log it in CRM or product analytics.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 5.3 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

3. **Track days from contract/signature to activation for each new customer** — review outliers in a monthly CS sync.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 5.3 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

4. **Iterate the checklist when activation slips** — update docs before hiring the next CS person.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 5.3 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

## Warning signs you're behind

### Output quality

- **Activation means something different to sales, product, and CS.**
  <!-- sources: processes.json 5.3 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

### Founder time

- **Every new customer still requires founder involvement to go live.**
  <!-- sources: processes.json 5.3 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

### Process entry

- **Customers stall in "implementation" with no named next step.**
  <!-- sources: processes.json 5.3 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->
