# Renewal & Retention — Early Revenue

> Knowing expiry dates was enough at [first hires](5.5--first-hires.md). Early revenue needs a renewal calendar, 90-day motion, at-risk signals, and churn as a core metric.

## What good looks like at this stage

- **Renewal calendar lists every contract end date and owner.**
  <!-- sources: processes.json 5.5 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

- **Renewal conversations start ~90 days before expiry** — not the week of.
  <!-- sources: processes.json 5.5 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

- **At-risk accounts flagged from support, usage, or NPS; churn rate tracked monthly.**
  <!-- sources: processes.json 5.5 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

## What you actually need to do

1. **Build renewal calendar in CRM or spreadsheet** — customer, ARR, end date, owner, status — update on every new deal.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 5.5 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

2. **At T-90 days, open renewal task:** health check, exec sponsor touch, proposal path — same checklist every time.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 5.5 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

3. **Define at-risk triggers** — e.g. NPS ≤6, no login 30 days, escalation ticket — and review list weekly with CS/sales.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 5.5 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

4. **Report logo and revenue churn monthly** in leadership and [investor updates](1.2b--early-revenue.md).
   <!-- sources: processes.json 5.5 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

## Warning signs you're behind

### Output quality

- **Renewals surprise you the month they happen.**
  <!-- sources: processes.json 5.5 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

### Founder time

- **Churn post-mortems only after the customer is gone.**
  <!-- sources: processes.json 5.5 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

### Process entry

- **Nobody owns renewals between sales close and finance invoice.**
  <!-- sources: processes.json 5.5 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->
