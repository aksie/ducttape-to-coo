# Customer Health, Engagement, Satisfaction, Support — Early Revenue

> Personal inbox support from [first hires](5.4--first-hires.md) becomes a channel, SLAs, satisfaction signal, and product feedback loop.

## What good looks like at this stage

- **Support runs through a dedicated tool or queue** — not lost in founder DMs.
  <!-- sources: processes.json 5.4 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

- **Response SLAs are defined and tracked.**
  <!-- sources: processes.json 5.4 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

- **NPS or CSAT runs at least quarterly; themes reach product systematically.**
  <!-- sources: processes.json 5.4 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

## What you actually need to do

1. **Move support to one system** (Intercom, Zendesk, Freshdesk, or shared support@ with ticketing) — publish how customers reach you.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 5.4 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

2. **Set SLAs by tier** — e.g. first response 4h business hours, resolution target by severity — and review weekly.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 5.4 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

3. **Send a simple quarterly NPS or CSAT survey** — tag detractors for follow-up within 48h.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 5.4 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

4. **Weekly product ↔ support sync:** top tickets, recurring bugs, feature requests — logged in one backlog, not Slack scroll.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 5.4 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

## Warning signs you're behind

### Output quality

- **Same bug reported by five customers before product hears about it.**
  <!-- sources: processes.json 5.4 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

### Founder time

- **Support still routes through founders' personal inboxes.**
  <!-- sources: processes.json 5.4 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

### Process entry

- **Customers don't know where to ask for help.**
  <!-- sources: processes.json 5.4 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->
