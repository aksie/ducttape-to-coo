# Customer Acquisition & Lead Management — Early Revenue

> Capturing leads in a board was enough at [first hires](5.2--first-hires.md). Early revenue needs qualification rules, routing, conversion metrics, and response SLAs.

## What good looks like at this stage

- **Lead qualification criteria are documented** — MQL/SQL or your equivalent, agreed by marketing and sales.
  <!-- sources: processes.json 5.2 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

- **Routing rules in the CRM assign leads automatically** — not "@sales who wants this?"
  <!-- sources: processes.json 5.2 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

- **Lead-to-close conversion and response times are measured weekly.**
  <!-- sources: processes.json 5.2 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

## What you actually need to do

1. **Write qualification criteria on one page** — firmographic, intent, budget, timeline — and reject or nurture below the bar.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 5.2 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

2. **Configure CRM routing** — round-robin, territory, or segment — with a fallback owner if unassigned >24h.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 5.2 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

3. **Track lead-to-opportunity and opportunity-to-close rates** — simple funnel report reviewed weekly.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 5.2 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

4. **Set response SLAs** (e.g. inbound lead contacted within 4 business hours) and monitor compliance.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 5.2 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

## Warning signs you're behind

### Output quality

- **Marketing celebrates lead volume; sales says leads are junk** — no shared definition.
  <!-- sources: processes.json 5.2 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

### Founder time

- **Hot inbound leads sit until a founder notices the form submission.**
  <!-- sources: processes.json 5.2 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

### Process entry

- **Routing is "whoever saw it first in Slack."**
  <!-- sources: processes.json 5.2 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->
