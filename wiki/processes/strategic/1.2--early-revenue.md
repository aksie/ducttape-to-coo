# Internal Communication about Strategy — Early Revenue

> At ~11–25 people the strategy story cannot ride on founder osmosis alone. Formal rhythm — all-hands, quarterly planning with input, retros — replaces "everyone still fits in one room."

## What good looks like at this stage

- **Monthly all-hands share progress, priorities, and real Q&A — not a status deck read at people.**
  The [first-hires habits](1.2--first-hires.md) (visible goals, weekly check-ins) scale into a company-wide forum; new hires who missed the origin story hear it here too.
  <!-- sources: src-064 (anonymous-practitioner), src-010 (firstround_review) -->

- **Quarterly planning includes team input before priorities are locked.**
  Not consensus theatre — but product, sales, and ops can surface constraints before the plan is final.
  <!-- sources: src-010 (firstround_review), src-026 -->

- **Retros or feedback sessions run on a cadence** — what to start, stop, change — so misalignment surfaces before it becomes attrition.
  <!-- sources: src-010 (firstround_review) -->

## What you actually need to do

1. **Hold a monthly all-hands (60–90 min):** last month's results vs quarterly goals, this month's focus, open Q&A. Record or write up answers that affect priorities.
   <!-- sources: src-064 (anonymous-practitioner) -->

2. **Before each quarter: one planning session with leads (and optionally the wider team) to stress-test the draft goals** from [1.1](1.1--early-revenue.md) — then publish the final list where [1.2 first-hires](1.2--first-hires.md) already says to keep priorities visible.
   <!-- sources: src-010 (firstround_review), src-026 -->

3. **Run a lightweight retro every month or six weeks** — start/stop/continue or equivalent — and assign one owner to act on the top item.
   <!-- sources: src-010 (firstround_review) -->

4. **After planning changes, repeat the "why" in all-hands and team meetings** — early revenue is where silent founder drift starts to split functions.
   <!-- sources: src-064 (anonymous-practitioner), src-026 -->

## Warning signs you're behind

### Output quality

- **Different functions explain company direction differently** — sales, product, and ops each have a different "top priority" when asked.
  <!-- sources: src-064 (anonymous-practitioner) -->

### Process entry

- **New hires learn strategy from their manager's version, not a shared forum.**
  All-hands skipped or treated as optional.
  <!-- sources: src-064 (anonymous-practitioner) -->

### Founder time

- **Quarterly goals are set in a founder offsite and never re-explained to the team.**
  Planning happened; communication did not.
  <!-- sources: src-064 (anonymous-practitioner) -->

## How this evolves next

At **growth**, downward transmission after every planning cycle becomes the main job — see [1.2 at Growth](1.2--growth.md). Assume most people heard the story once, not once a week.
