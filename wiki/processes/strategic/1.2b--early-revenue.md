# Shareholder & Investor Reporting — Early Revenue

> The monthly email habit from [first hires](1.2b--first-hires.md) continues — early revenue adds board rhythm, cap table hygiene, and legal coordination on anything that touches shareholders.

## What good looks like at this stage

- **Monthly investor updates still go out on calendar cadence** — same template, honest lookback — now to angels, seed investors, or an informal board.
  <!-- sources: src-058 (practitioner contribution, human:stefanverkerk), src-059 (template synthesis) -->

- **Board or advisor meetings have a deck and pre-read sent in advance** — not slides assembled in the room.
  <!-- sources: src-058 (human:stefanverkerk), processes.json 1.2b stageFocus -->

- **Cap table is current and reconcilable** — options, SAFEs, and side letters don't live only in a lawyer's inbox.
  <!-- sources: src-058 (human:stefanverkerk) -->

## What you actually need to do

1. **Keep the monthly [investor update](../../../templates/rendered/investor-update.html)** — add quarterly runway/burn/scenario sections once per quarter as in the template.
   <!-- sources: src-058 (human:stefanverkerk), src-059 -->

2. **For each board or advisor session: send a pre-read 48 hours ahead** — deck plus the same metrics as the monthly update, plus asks for the meeting.
   <!-- sources: src-058 (human:stefanverkerk) -->

3. **Maintain cap table in one system of record** (Carta, Ledgy, spreadsheet with legal sign-off) — update within a week of any issuance or conversion.
   <!-- sources: src-058 (human:stefanverkerk) -->

4. **Loop legal in before any shareholder-facing action** — new round, option pool, director change, or side letter — not after signatures.
   <!-- sources: src-058 (human:stefanverkerk), processes.json 1.2b stageFocus -->

## Warning signs you're behind

### Output quality

- **Board meetings are the first time investors see the numbers** — no pre-read, no consistent metrics month to month.
  <!-- sources: src-058 (human:stefanverkerk) -->

### Process entry

- **Nobody can produce a current cap table without asking the lawyer.**
  <!-- sources: src-058 (human:stefanverkerk) -->

### Founder time

- **Shareholder questions get answered in ad hoc WhatsApp threads** instead of the monthly written record.
  <!-- sources: src-058 (human:stefanverkerk) -->

## How this evolves next

At **growth**: structured board cadence, formal board packs, and emerging investor-relations discipline — see [1.2b at Growth](1.2b--growth.md).
