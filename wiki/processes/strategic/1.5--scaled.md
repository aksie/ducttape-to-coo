# Organizational Design — Scaled (51+ people)

> Executives run the day. Two jobs are left: the story, and turning the numbers into what the company actually does. The dollar bands in the source are that post's, not a new set of stages.

## What good looks like at this stage

- **Two jobs remain once executives run the day-to-day: carrying the story, and turning finance into operations.** One tells the story, recruits, and sells. The other knows what will move the business and turns that into results.
  <!-- sources: src-124 (fund partner, vc_portfolio_interest) -->

- **The story still works when people who have never met you tell it.** Most of the company will not hear it from you. If it only works in your voice, it does not travel.
  <!-- sources: src-124 (fund partner, vc_portfolio_interest) -->

## What you actually need to do

1. **If you are not excellent at turning finance into operations, hire a partner for that role.** Do not try to hire someone to be the story in your place. That role is the hard one to fill.
   <!-- sources: src-124 (fund partner, vc_portfolio_interest) -->

## Warning signs you're behind

### Founder / key-person time

- **You are still the only person who can say what will move the business, or the only person who can tell the story.** The company has executives and still depends on one person for the point of the work or for the numbers.
  <!-- sources: src-124 (fund partner, vc_portfolio_interest) -->
