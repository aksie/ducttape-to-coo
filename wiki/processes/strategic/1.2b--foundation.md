# Shareholder & Investor Reporting — Foundation

> Written reporting is for **you** first — external investors are an audience layer on a habit that keeps you honest. Start monthly before you have shareholders; widen distribution when angels arrive.

## What good looks like at this stage

- **A monthly written update runs on calendar cadence.**
  Recipients are only you and co-founders — no external investors required. Same structure you will send shareholders later. This scores [1.1 quarterly priorities](1.1--foundation.md) in writing, not just in standups.
  <!-- sources: src-058 (practitioner contribution, human:stefanverkerk) -->

- **Each update records what went differently than expected.**
  Highlights and misses up front; checkbox lookback against the goals you stated last month. Performing confidence is not the point — documenting reality is.
  <!-- sources: src-058 (human:stefanverkerk), src-059 (template synthesis) -->

## What you actually need to do

1. **Send the first edition to yourself or co-founders this month.**
   Use the [investor update template](../../../templates/rendered/investor-update.html): highlights, **misses/help up front**, checkbox lookback, checkbox lookahead. Quarterly runway and scenario sections can wait.
   <!-- sources: src-058 (human:stefanverkerk), src-059, templates/docs/investor-update.md -->

2. **On lookback, explain the delta — not just checkboxes.**
   For each goal from [1.1 priorities](1.1--foundation.md) or your prior update: what you did, what outcome differed from plan, what you learned.
   <!-- sources: src-058 (human:stefanverkerk) -->

3. **Keep the cadence when there is no external audience.**
   Verbal alignment in founder syncs is not enough — coffee conversations let you repeat "ships in six weeks" without confronting the slip in writing.
   <!-- sources: src-058 (human:stefanverkerk) -->

## Warning signs you're behind

### Founder / key-person time

- **Alignment happens only in verbal standups or coffees.**
  You have said "ships in six weeks" three times without a written record of the slip.
  <!-- sources: src-058 (human:stefanverkerk) -->

### Process entry

- **You cannot point to last month's stated goals and how you scored against them.**
  Priorities live in [1.1](1.1--foundation.md) but nothing forces an honest monthly score.
  <!-- sources: src-058 (human:stefanverkerk), src-059 (template synthesis) -->

## How this evolves next

At **first hires**, keep the same monthly habit — add explicit asks when helpful, widen BCC when angels arrive, and tie lookback to [quarterly goals](../../../templates/rendered/quarterly-goals.html) — see [1.2b at First Hires](1.2b--first-hires.md).

## Context variants

### If you are bootstrapped (no external investors yet)

- **Audience is internal only — that is correct.**
  Founder-only or leadership distribution still counts. The discipline is writing the lookback; investors are not the reason to start.
  <!-- sources: src-058 (human:stefanverkerk) -->

### If you have angel or friends & family investors

- **Same monthly template, wider distribution when they are on the cap table.**
  If angels exist already at foundation, add them to the same email — do not invent a new format under pressure.
  <!-- sources: src-058 (human:stefanverkerk) -->
