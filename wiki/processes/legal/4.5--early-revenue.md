# Internal & Labour — Early Revenue

> At early revenue the question shifts from "do we have a template?" to "can we produce every signed contract and amendment in an hour?" — investors and enterprise customers will ask.

## What good looks like at this stage

- **Every freelancer and contractor runs on the same reviewed template — not one-off agreements.**
  Explicit IP assignment and wet-DBA indemnity on every engagement, same as [first hires](4.5--first-hires.md). Ad hoc contracts are how IP and schijnzelfstandigheid risk creep back in.
  <!-- sources: src-061 (Trailhead legal scan, human:trailhead-legal) -->

- **Signed employment contracts and filed addenda are findable without a scavenger hunt.**
  When diligence or a customer security review asks for headcount vs contract reconciliation, you pull a folder — not Slack threads and inboxes.
  <!-- sources: src-061 (Trailhead legal scan, human:trailhead-legal) -->

## What you actually need to do

1. **Freeze one contractor/freelance template and use it for every new engagement.**
   Same IP-transfer and indemnity clauses as your first-hires baseline; no "just this once" side letters unless filed as addenda.
   <!-- sources: src-061 (Trailhead legal scan, human:trailhead-legal) -->

2. **Before each new contractor starts, run the template — not a negotiated one-off.**
   If terms must differ, write an addendum, sign it, and file it next to the master agreement.
   <!-- sources: src-061 (Trailhead legal scan, human:trailhead-legal) -->

3. **Audit the employment folder quarterly:** every employee has a signed contract; every pay change has a matching addendum on file.
   Spot-check that payroll totals reconcile to contract + addenda — the €90k vs €75k gap shows up here first.
   <!-- sources: src-061 (Trailhead legal scan, human:trailhead-legal) -->

4. **Keep contracts in the [Company Ops Registry](../../../templates/rendered/company-ops-registry.html) layout** — one obvious path per person, current signed PDF only.
   <!-- sources: src-061 (Trailhead legal scan, human:trailhead-legal) -->

## Warning signs you're behind

### Output quality

- **You used different freelancer agreements for different projects and cannot say which IP clause each person signed.**
  Due diligence treats inconsistent contractor paper as a controls gap, not a formatting issue.
  <!-- sources: src-061 (Trailhead legal scan, human:trailhead-legal) -->

### Process entry

- **A diligence or customer questionnaire asks for "all employment contracts" and the team spends days assembling them.**
  The process failed at filing, not at signing.
  <!-- sources: src-061 (Trailhead legal scan, human:trailhead-legal) -->

### Founder time

- **Side deals on pay or hours live in email but not in filed addenda.**
  You will renegotiate under pressure or fail reconciliation when someone external looks.
  <!-- sources: src-061 (Trailhead legal scan, human:trailhead-legal) -->

## How this evolves next

- **At growth, the failure mode becomes template vs actual pay drift across many hires — move to an HRIS and keep documented terms true to what's paid.**
  See [Internal & Labour — Growth](4.5--growth.md).
