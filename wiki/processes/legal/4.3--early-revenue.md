# IT Administration & User Support — Early Revenue

> Tool sprawl and manual provisioning break at early revenue. Lock a core stack, document new-hire setup, and wire deprovisioning into [offboarding](3.4--early-revenue.md).

## What good looks like at this stage

- **Core tech stack is defined and enforced** — new tools need a reason, not a credit card.
  <!-- sources: processes.json 4.3 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

- **New-employee IT setup is a written runbook** — accounts, devices, 2FA, password manager — same steps every hire.
  <!-- sources: processes.json 4.3 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

- **Deprovisioning is part of offboarding, not an afterthought.**
  <!-- sources: src-051 (hacker_news), processes.json 4.3 stageFocus seed -->

## What you actually need to do

1. **Publish a "standard stack" list** — identity (Google/M365), chat, docs, CRM, finance, password manager — and a rule for adding exceptions.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 4.3 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

2. **Write a one-page new-hire IT checklist** linked from [3.3 onboarding](3.3--early-revenue.md): create account, groups, MFA, laptop image, password manager vault access.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 4.3 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

3. **Maintain the master access list from [3.4 first hires](3.4--first-hires.md)** — update on join and revoke on leave the same day.
   <!-- sources: src-051 (hacker_news) -->

4. **Quarterly: review SaaS seats vs active employees** — remove orphaned accounts and unused tools.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 4.3 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

## Warning signs you're behind

### Output quality

- **Offboarding completes but former employees still appear in Slack, GitHub, or admin panels.**
  <!-- sources: src-051 (hacker_news), src-053 (hacker_news) -->

### Process entry

- **New hires wait days for basic access** because setup lives in one person's head.
  <!-- sources: processes.json 4.3 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

### Founder time

- **Every tool has its own login model and nobody knows the full list.**
  <!-- sources: src-051 (hacker_news) -->

## How this evolves next

At **growth**: IT owner or MSP, SSO, automated provisioning, and ticketed support — see [4.3 at Growth](4.3--growth.md).
