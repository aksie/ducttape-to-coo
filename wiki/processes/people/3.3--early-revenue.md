# Onboarding — Early Revenue

> At early revenue, onboarding cannot depend on whoever remembers the last hire. Checklist on paper, 30-day check-in, and role-specific ramp — building on [first hires](3.3--first-hires.md).

## What good looks like at this stage

- **Onboarding checklist is written, versioned, and owned** — not tribal knowledge in one ops-minded founder.
  <!-- sources: src-014 (firstround_review), src-016 (gitlab_handbook) -->

- **Every new hire gets a 30-day check-in** — manager plus a neutral listener if possible — to catch misfires before they calcify.
  <!-- sources: src-014 (firstround_review), processes.json 3.3 stageFocus seed -->

- **Role-specific ramp plans exist for common hires** — what "good" looks like at 30/60/90 days for engineer, seller, CS, etc.
  <!-- sources: processes.json 3.3 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

## What you actually need to do

1. **Move the [first-hires checklist](3.3--first-hires.md) into a doc in your handbook** — pre-day-one, day one, week one — and update it after every hire ("leave it better").
   <!-- sources: src-014 (firstround_review), src-016 (gitlab_handbook) -->

2. **Schedule a 30-day check-in on day one** — same questions every time: clarity, tools, manager, culture fit, surprises.
   <!-- sources: src-014 (firstround_review) -->

3. **Write one ramp plan per recurring role** — milestones, buddies, first projects — so managers aren't reinventing onboarding per hire.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 3.3 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

4. **Assign an onboarding buddy outside the direct manager** for every hire — carries culture and navigation from first hires practice.
   <!-- sources: src-015 (firstround_review) -->

## Warning signs you're behind

### Output quality

- **Last three hires had materially different first weeks** because nobody owns the checklist.
  <!-- sources: src-014 (firstround_review) -->

### Process entry

- **New hires still chase laptop, accounts, or calendar on day one.**
  <!-- sources: src-014 (firstround_review), src-016 (gitlab_handbook) -->

### Founder time

- **Founders still deliver every onboarding personally** — doesn't scale and hides broken process.
  <!-- sources: src-014 (firstround_review) -->

## How this evolves next

At **growth**: onboarding runs without the founder — structured week, buddies, 30-60-90 tracking — see [3.3 at Growth](3.3--growth.md).
