# Employee Satisfaction & Feedback — Early Revenue

> Weekly start/stop/change in the team sync isn't enough at ~15–25 people. Regular 1-on-1s and a light monthly pulse catch issues before they become departures.

## What good looks like at this stage

- **Every employee has a recurring 1-on-1 with their manager** — not skipped when busy.
  <!-- sources: processes.json 3.6 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

- **A simple monthly pulse (e.g. 1–5 "how are you feeling about work?") adds trend signal** without a full engagement survey.
  <!-- sources: processes.json 3.6 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

## What you actually need to do

1. **Calendar a bi-weekly or weekly 1-on-1 for every report** — standing agenda: priorities, blockers, feedback, career — 25–30 minutes.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 3.6 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

2. **Send one monthly pulse question to all hands** (Slack form, Google Form, or HR tool) — same question each month so you see trends.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 3.6 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

3. **Review pulse and exit themes together quarterly** with [3.4 offboarding](3.4--early-revenue.md) insights — one owner assigns fixes.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 3.6 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

4. **Keep the first-hires start/stop/change in team meetings** — team-level feedback plus individual 1-on-1s cover different gaps.
   <!-- sources: processes.json 3.6 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

## Warning signs you're behind

### Output quality

- **Surprises at resignation** — "I had no idea they were unhappy."
  <!-- sources: processes.json 3.6 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

### Founder time

- **1-on-1s happen only when someone asks for a raise or threatens to leave.**
  <!-- sources: processes.json 3.6 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->

### Process entry

- **Managers don't know they're expected to run 1-on-1s** — no norm documented.
  <!-- sources: processes.json 3.6 stageFocus seed | flags: unverified -->
