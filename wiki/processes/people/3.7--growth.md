# Learning & Development — Growth

> The first months are how new people learn the craft and the company. Leaders teach part of it themselves.

## What good looks like at this stage

- **Every new hire spends the first months in an in-house training program, about half on the craft and half on working with people.** Leadership give part of that training to spread culture and know all the new hires.
  <!-- sources: src-117 (commercial-operator, people_business) -->

## What you actually need to do

1. **Leaders teach most of it.** That keeps them close to new people and shows who is growing. Buy outside teaching only for depth you do not have.
   <!-- sources: src-117 (commercial-operator, people_business) -->
