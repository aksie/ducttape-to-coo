# Offboarding — Early Revenue

> More hires means more departures. The [first-hires checklist](3.4--first-hires.md) becomes non-optional: same steps every time, and exit insights loop back to hiring and management.

## What good looks like at this stage

- **The offboarding checklist runs for every departure** — voluntary, involuntary, contractor end — with no "we were too busy" skips.
  <!-- sources: src-056 (openorg.fyi), src-057 (tinyteam) -->

- **Exit interview themes are captured and reviewed quarterly** — patterns feed [3.2 hiring](3.2--early-revenue.md) and manager coaching, not just HR files.
  <!-- sources: src-056 (openorg.fyi), processes.json 3.4 stageFocus seed -->

## What you actually need to do

1. **Keep the first-hires checklist current** — IT revoke order, equipment return, handover template, exit interview — and assign a single run owner per departure.
   <!-- sources: src-056 (openorg.fyi), src-057 (tinyteam) -->

2. **Log exit interview answers in one place** (even a spreadsheet) tagged by manager, role, and theme — review in a quarterly people ops sync.
   <!-- sources: src-056 (openorg.fyi), processes.json 3.4 stageFocus seed -->

3. **Tie offboarding to [4.3 IT](4.3--early-revenue.md) deprovisioning** — departure confirmed same day triggers access removal per checklist.
   <!-- sources: src-053 (hacker_news), src-057 (tinyteam) -->

4. **For each theme that repeats (manager, comp, role clarity), assign one fix** — update job spec, manager 1:1 cadence, or onboarding — before the next hire.
   <!-- sources: src-056 (openorg.fyi), processes.json 3.4 stageFocus seed -->

## Warning signs you're behind

### Output quality

- **Second departure in a quarter and you're rebuilding the process from scratch.**
  <!-- sources: src-057 (tinyteam) -->

### Process entry

- **Exit interviews happen but notes sit in a private doc nobody reads.**
  <!-- sources: src-056 (openorg.fyi) -->

### Founder time

- **Access revocation still depends on remembering which tools someone used.**
  Master access list from first hires not maintained.
  <!-- sources: src-051 (hacker_news) -->

## How this evolves next

At **growth**: formal offboarding owner, automated access reviews, and structured alumni process — see [3.4 at Growth](3.4--growth.md).
