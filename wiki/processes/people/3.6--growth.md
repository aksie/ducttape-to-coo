# Employee Satisfaction & Feedback — Growth

> At growth, the survey has to measure the company you said you want. A company-wide number is not the conversation.

## What good looks like at this stage

The survey matches the philosophy you already stated, and each team can talk about its own score.

## What you actually need to do

1. **Pick the employee satisfaction survey that matches the philosophy you already stated.** They chose a pride-oriented instrument after deciding work happiness was the point. A survey that measures a different company becomes a second strategy. A sector-specific variant is mentioned in the transcript and is not clear enough to copy.
   <!-- sources: src-117 (commercial-operator, people_business) -->

2. **Report the score per team and use it as the conversation with that team.** The results here were predictable. They still gave a basis for the talk with each team. A company-wide number hides the cell that is sliding.
   <!-- sources: src-117 (commercial-operator, people_business) -->
