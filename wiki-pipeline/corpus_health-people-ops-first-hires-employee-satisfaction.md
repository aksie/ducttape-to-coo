## Cell-specific finding

The cell is not empty, and it is not a survey. At 2–10 people the satisfaction process is a conversation the leader can still have with everyone. The maintainer's monthly walk or lunch matches the practitioner pattern for the deeper questions (like, dislike, morale, unused talent). Practitioners add three limits: do not cancel it, do not turn it into status, and do not save this week's friction for next month's lunch. A dissent says that while everyone still talks all day, the formal round should stay light.
