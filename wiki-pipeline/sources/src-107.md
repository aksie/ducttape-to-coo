## Summary

Kaiser and Kaplan’s Leadership Versatility Index work: strengths overused become weaknesses. Leaders who succeed through a dominant style double down on it under stress, creating the very problems the next stage requires them to solve differently. Empirical framing under Turner’s “default mode becomes kryptonite.”

## Notable claims

- Strengths overused turn into liabilities
- Under pressure, leaders amplify their preferred style rather than flex
- Versatility (dialling up and down complementary behaviours) predicts effectiveness better than raw strength
- “Do more of what you’re good at” is bad advice at role transitions
