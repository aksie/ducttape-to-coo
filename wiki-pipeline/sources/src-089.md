## Summary

HN thread (Nov 2021) discussing how boards of VC-backed companies are "presented too positively" in founder-facing advice, when the reality is that boards carry real, sometimes adversarial power over founder-CEOs. Discussion covers CEO removal mechanics and how differently that plays out depending on board composition and cap table structure. Search-engine-indexed summary only; direct fetch of news.ycombinator.com is blocked in this environment, so treat as a lower-fidelity secondary read of the thread.

## Notable claims

- The board's legal power to remove a CEO exists in essentially all VC-backed companies, not just ones with unusually founder-unfriendly terms
- Common framing that "the board works for the founder" understates the board's actual authority once institutional investors hold seats
- Board danger to founders is highest exactly when it's least visible day-to-day — during calm periods trust and formal power look the same, and founders conflate them
