## Summary

Meta-analysis of workplace coaching studies finding positive effects on learning and performance outcomes. Complements Theeboom et al. as a second meta-analytic pillar for coaching as an intervention when helping founders change leadership behaviour.

## Notable claims

- Workplace coaching positively affects learning outcomes
- Workplace coaching positively affects performance outcomes
- Supports coaching as evidence-backed support for leadership transitions
- Still does not replace naming owners and giving away jobs — coaching enables the identity shift that makes those structural moves stick
