## Summary

Classic Organization Science article on the tension between exploitation (refining what already works) and exploration (searching for new alternatives). Organisations and individuals systematically favour exploitation because returns are clearer and nearer; that competence trap undermines the exploration needed to adapt when the environment or role demands change. Directly relevant to founders who keep leading from the mode that already won.

## Notable claims

- Exploitation of known competence crowds out exploration of new capabilities
- Returns to exploitation are more certain and nearer-term, so people and orgs over-invest in it
- Competence traps: success at current practices reduces willingness to learn new ones
- Adaptation requires deliberate investment in exploration even when it feels inefficient
