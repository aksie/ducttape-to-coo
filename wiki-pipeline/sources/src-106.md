## Summary

Ibarra’s longitudinal work on leadership transitions (*Working Identity*, 2003; *Act Like a Leader, Think Like a Leader*, 2015): people do not change identity by deciding first and acting later. They change by experimenting with new behaviours, networks, and self-narratives — “act like a leader” before the identity feels natural. Directly counters “I’ll delegate when I feel ready / when I trust them.”

## Notable claims

- Identity transitions lag role transitions; waiting to feel like a leader delays the change
- Effective change uses experiments (new behaviours and relationships), not insight alone
- Outsight (learning from action and external networks) beats introspection for leadership identity shifts
- Founders stuck in doer identity need practice of leader behaviours before the self-story updates
