## Summary

2019 essay by the founder of SoapBox, a product for manager 1:1s and team meetings. The commercial interest is direct: he argues surveys should be replaced by better manager conversations, then points at the product. Useful anyway for the stage claim that under about ten people the leader is the manager, so a survey score is a special case, and for the mechanism that a slow survey loop returns harsh feedback after people have already decided to leave. Treat the anti-survey conclusion as vendor-aligned. The small-team anonymity problem is corroborated by the size of the team itself.

## Notable claims

- Under about ten people, engagement looks high because the leader is the manager
- An average hides the person; a conversation can follow up
- A slow survey loop misses people who leave
- Do not delegate satisfaction to a form you do not have an HR team to run
