## Summary

Quantitative study (first online 2013; issue 2016) comparing supervisors receiving Immunity-to-Change coaching (n≈45) with a control group (n≈25). Coached group reported significantly more progress toward leadership development goals. Provides empirical backing that ITC-style coaching can move leadership behaviour, not just insight.

## Notable claims

- ITC coaching produced more progress on leadership development goals than a comparison group
- Structured coaching against competing commitments can shift behaviour
- Evidence base for ITC as an intervention is positive but still a single substantive study — treat as promising, not settled
