## Summary

Ask HN thread on how managers run 1:1s. Practitioners describe the slot as the employee's time, warn against cancelling it, and separate project status from the conversation about the person. Several mention a walk or coffee as the setting. One describes a monthly tea specifically for morale, happiness, and personal progress, with shorter check-ins in between, and a rule that urgent feedback does not wait for the slot. Managers of about ten to thirteen people describe the time cost, and report that people often prefer every two weeks over weekly.

## Notable claims

- The employee's time; no status
- Do not cancel
- Listen; advise only when asked
- Walk or coffee is a valid room
- Monthly morale conversation, with faster feedback in between
- Weekly with everyone is a heavy load around ten people
