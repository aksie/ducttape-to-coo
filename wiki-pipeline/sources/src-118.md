## Summary

Stefan Verkerk, 17 November 2026, proposing the first-hires shape of employee satisfaction before a survey exists. Someone from leadership takes a walk or a lunch once a month with each person and talks about what they currently like and do not like, how happy and engaged they feel, and whether the company is letting them use their full talent.

Numbering starts at src-118 so it does not collide with src-117 on the commercial-operator branch.

## Notable claims

- Monthly, not weekly, at 2–10 people
- A walk or a lunch, with someone from leadership
- Four questions: like, dislike, happiness and engagement, full use of talent
- This is a proposal to check against practitioner sources, not a completed practice
