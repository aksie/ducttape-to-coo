## Summary

SignalFire blog describing the **Quarterly Sit-Down (QSD)** — a one-page review format with **15–20 minutes prep** per side. Employee and manager independently complete identical forms, **swap at the meeting without pre-sharing**, read silently, then discuss misalignment. Fields cover accomplishments, top five goals, strengths, development areas, manager support, and upward feedback. Hold **first week of the new quarter**. Assumes continuous feedback and regular check-ins between QSDs; documents disagreements in the form for trail.

## Notable claims

- QSD replaces multipage writeups; prep and delivery each under ~15 minutes after practice
- Identical form both sides; no sharing before in-person swap — misalignment surfaces immediately
- Form prompts: key accomplishments, top 5 next-quarter goals, top 5 strengths, 3 development areas, 3 ways manager can help, feedback for manager
- Quarterly cadence, ideally week one after quarter close
- Not a replacement for KPI tracking or continuous 1-on-1 feedback — diagnostic on why goals did/didn't land
- Manager notes disagreements on form for documentation; comp/promo reviews can summarize QSD trail
