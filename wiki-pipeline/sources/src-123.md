## Summary

Thread comment arguing that in startups under about fifty people everyone already talks to everyone, so a special meeting to extract what is wrong is unnecessary. Replies disagree: a scheduled conversation is how you hear what people will not say in the open, and it remains useful inside a small team. Registered as the dissent against installing a formal round too early.

## Notable claims

- A formal slot can be ceremony while the hallway still works
- The counter-test is whether worries arrive without an appointment
