## Summary

HN thread on Kent Beck’s response to Paul Graham’s “founder mode.” Useful adversarial balance for this cell: convincing founders to change is not the same as “never touch the work.” Commenters warn against cargo-culting micromanagement because famous founders sometimes dive deep; also warn against the false dichotomy of either full hands-off professional management or constant countermanding.

## Notable claims

- “Hire good people and leave them alone” vs “founder mode” is a false dichotomy — aim at the outcome, not at deviation for its own sake
- Micromanaging because successful founders sometimes dive deep is cargo culting
- Founder involvement that undermines managers after structure exists destroys scale
- Advice from outlier founders needs context; novices cargo-culting their habits fails
