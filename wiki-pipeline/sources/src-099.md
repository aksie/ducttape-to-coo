## Summary

First Round notepad of operator quotes framed around when founders should stop being head of product. Highest-signal lines for this cell: Molly Graham — grow as fast as the company by giving away your job every couple of months; Sam Corcos — CEO builds fire departments, does not put out fires; Sean Twersky — “I trust you, make the call”; Alexis Ohanian — every non-code/non-user moment needs a conscious worth-your-time test.

## Notable claims

- Give away your job every couple of months if you want to grow as fast as the company (Molly Graham)
- Build organisational capacity to handle fires; don’t personally extinguish them (Corcos)
- Trust language that transfers the call is a leadership lever (Twersky)
- Founder time away from product/users must be intentional, not default ops absorption (Ohanian)
