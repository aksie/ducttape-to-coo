## Summary

HN thread (2017, 100+ comments) on performance appraisal at startups. Strong practitioner signal for **~20-person** companies: quarterly OKRs plus regular 1:1s, metrics to set expectations rather than as final ratings, optional 360 feedback, and first review timing (~9 months then yearly). Consensus that early-stage startups skip heavy formalities — but midsize and 20-person teams still need lightweight structure.

## Notable claims

- At a ~20-person company: quarterly objectives plus regular 1:1s; metrics set expectations, not final ratings
- Strongest performance tool is 1:1s — weekly/bi-weekly with manager; can't wait for once- or twice-yearly career conversations
- First review ~9 months after joining, then yearly — waiting a full year for first salary review is too long
- 360 feedback useful for highlighting contributions top-down review misses and surfacing improvement areas — gameable but patterns visible
- Early-stage startups often have no time for formalities; regular 1:1s are the best option until scale demands more
