## Summary

First Round Review essay by a former Airbnb product lead on an end-to-end manager performance framework used across 50+ reviews. Separates **weekly 1:1s** (execution) from **monthly career coaching** (growth). Uses a simple shared spreadsheet of development goals updated before each career meeting. Emphasizes manager prep and personalization rather than perfunctory corporate forms.

## Notable claims

- Schedule monthly "career coaching" distinct from weekly 1:1s — no project status; focus on career and performance development
- After review cycles, employee maintains a simple spreadsheet of growth goals; update before each monthly career meeting
- Manager must prep — don't take forms and talking points from on high and have a perfunctory conversation
- System maps onto company-wide review cycles but allows bespoke manager approach that persists through org changes
- Framework used to grow junior ICs into strong performers and address trouble spots systematically
