## Summary

HN thread (2017, 400+ comments) comparing performance review approaches across company sizes. Relevant patterns for early-revenue: quarterly check-ins with self/peer input, **real-time feedback between cycles** so formal results aren't surprises, continue/stop/start 360-style questions, and small teams using yearly goals plus subjective manager/peer aggregation without heavy metrics.

## Notable claims

- Quarterly check-ins with peer and self reviews; real-time feedback between cycles so results are never a surprise
- Continue / stop / start questions in quarterly 360-style reviews; team leads hold 1:1s at least twice monthly
- Small company: yearly goals, subjective reviews from superiors and coworkers — works when excellence doesn't require "playing the social game"
- Rating scales removed at some companies because they didn't improve fairness or outcomes
- Annual-only negative feedback at review time means the manager failed — feedback delayed is feedback denied
