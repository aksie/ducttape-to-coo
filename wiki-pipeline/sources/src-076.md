## Summary

HN thread (2018) on team feedback culture. Key stage boundary: under ~10 people, talk openly and act on what you hear; **between ~15 and 25 people** informal feedback stops scaling — you need scheduled time, notes, and light documentation, and should expand who participates in the feedback loop beyond one founder. Over ~50, dedicated HR becomes mandatory.

## Notable claims

- Under 10 people: skip formal systems — talk regularly, listen, act or explain why not
- 10–25 people: need formality — schedule, documentation, follow-up; don't build a grand review system first
- Expand the feedback loop so one person isn't doing most of the cycle
- ~35–40 hire HR help; over 50 mandatory as legal employee responsibilities shift
- Pilot feedback systems often fail until paired with coaching/1:1 structure
