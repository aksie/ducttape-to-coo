## Summary

Engineering manager's own question set, updated through 2026. Ordinary 1:1s stay open. About once a month he asks a stay set built from Christopher Lee's performance questions and Kimberly Franklin's stay-interview questions. The set includes what is going well, what is not, unused talents, and the one thing they would change. He added a 0–10 burnout question because people do not volunteer overload, and he removed a goals-status question because it repeated the daily standup.

## Notable claims

- Monthly stay questions inside an otherwise open 1:1
- Unused talents, and one change
- Burnout as a number, asked rather than waited for
- Status questions push the stay questions out
