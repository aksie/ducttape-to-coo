## Summary

Practitioner note (2026-09-15): McKeown’s point that leadership may take a year to see Whitewater as more than bumps in the road shows up in the field as “it’s been a rough quarter.” Separately — and especially past a certain headcount — attrition and temporary absences are *always* opening holes. Designing process documentation and ownership as if the same people will always be present is the failure mode; the constant churn of leave/quits is the design constraint.

## Notable claims

- Leadership often labels Whitewater symptoms as a temporary rough quarter
- At scale, people leaving (and the holes that creates) is a constant, not an exception
- Process/docs/ownership must be designed for someone always being out
- If every departure feels like a crisis, maturity never left informal ownership
