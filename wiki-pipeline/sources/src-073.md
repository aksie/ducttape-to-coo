## Summary

Ask HN thread (Aug 2022) from a ~7-person fintech founder on employee transparency. High practitioner density on **sharing financials with early teams** — cash in bank, burn, runway — as trust-building and alignment, not a leak risk at small scale.

## Notable claims

- Multiple founders at ~7–10 people share cash, burn, runway openly with the team
- Open cash/scorecard lets non-founders spot problems founders missed
- Employees without cash visibility plug in worst-case assumptions and may leave
- Monthly business review with whole company including cash flow (at slightly larger scale)
