## Summary

Psychological Review paper arguing that self-worth is contingent on success in domains people have staked their identity on. Threats to those domains trigger defensiveness and resistance to change. Useful mechanism for why founders protect Producer/Entrepreneur identity (and status from early wins) even when the organisation now needs Administrator behaviour.

## Notable claims

- Self-worth is contingent on performance in personally important domains
- Threats to contingent domains produce defensiveness, not learning
- Status-linked identities are hard to relinquish because they regulate self-worth
- Change that asks people to abandon a success domain feels like identity loss, not a skill upgrade
