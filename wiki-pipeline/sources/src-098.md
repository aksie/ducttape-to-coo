## Summary

Solo SaaS founder asks how to move from doing everything to delegating and leading. Top answers: make yourself redundant daily (“fire yourself”); only keep irreversible/catastrophic decisions; ask where you’re most vs least valuable; delegate domains where someone else can master more than you, that are decoupled enough for mission intent, and deep enough for ongoing purpose. Explicit warning: do not delegate urgent-but-unimportant work — it burns social capital.

## Notable claims

- Fire yourself every day by making yourself redundant; hire yourself tomorrow for the next job
- If a decision need not be absolutely yours, delegate; teach others by exposing your decision criteria
- Prefer reversible, non-catastrophic decisions for early delegation practice
- Delegate domains others can master deeper than you, with autonomy and ongoing purpose
- Do not delegate urgent-yet-unimportant tasks — creates stressed interruptions about work you don’t care about
