## Summary

Canopy CEO answering a co-founder at a company under 150 people who was unsure about skip-level 1:1s. She sells leadership coaching, so the piece points back at Canopy. The operational parts that survive the sales frame: use the time for morale (energy, clarity, what they would change about the leaders), agree the purpose when two leaders both hold the conversations, and set the frequency from what you hear. Monthly is common. Quarterly is enough when the answers are already clear. Vague answers mean meet more often.

## Notable claims

- Morale questions: energy, direction, what to change about leadership
- Two leaders need a shared purpose or people hear two versions
- Frequency follows the quality of the answers
