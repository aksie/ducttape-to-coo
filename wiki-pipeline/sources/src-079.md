## Summary

Practitioner pattern for weekly manager 1-on-1s at early-revenue scale: one recurring doc per report with fixed weekly sections, room for the report to add agenda points, a standing **Feedback** item (both directions), and post-meeting notes of what was actually said. The log is the prep material for quarterly or annual performance conversations — formal reviews summarize documented feedback, not memory.

## Notable claims

- Same standing agenda every week; direct reports add items before the meeting
- Standing **Feedback** slot — from report to manager and manager to report
- Log feedback exchanged in the 1-on-1 doc after each meeting
- Written trail is what you refer back to at review time; avoids "I never heard that" disputes
- Without logging, formal reviews reconstruct performance from memory and feel like surprises
