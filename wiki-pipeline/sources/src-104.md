## Summary

Kegan and Lahey’s Immunity to Change framework: people can sincerely commit to a change goal and still fail because of hidden competing commitments and big assumptions that protect them from feared consequences. The immunity map surfaces those commitments so behaviour can shift. Central intervention model for “founder agrees they should delegate / stop duct-taping ops, then doesn’t.”

## Notable claims

- Stated commitment to change often coexists with competing commitments that block it
- Competing commitments protect against feared losses (status, control, identity)
- Insight alone rarely produces behaviour change; the immunity must be diagnosed
- Mapping big assumptions makes adaptive leadership change coachable
