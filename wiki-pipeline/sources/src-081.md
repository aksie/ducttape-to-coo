## Summary

Practitioner post on running lightweight performance reviews at a **~20-person** startup without HR. Defines informal **3–4 performance levels** and five behavioral dimensions before reviewing anyone. Three-step cycle: **self-review** (~30 min prompts), **manager prep** (~30–45 min with evidence from PRs/teammates, including "would you rehire at current salary?"), **review conversation** (45–60 min). Batch all reviews in a **two-week window**; **follow up in three months** on growth areas. Warns against cargo-cult 360s and vague positive reviews.

## Notable claims

- Without structure: feedback turns personal not behavioral; compensation drifts; people blindsided at PIP time
- Define 3–4 informal levels + what "solid code," ownership, collaboration, growth look like — share with new hires
- Self-review: proud of, harder than expected, growth wants, support needed
- Manager prep from PRs/Slack/teammate input, not memory; rehire-at-current-salary test
- Conversation order: specific wins → growth opportunities → concrete support → comp clarity
- Schedule every review within two weeks; 3-month follow-up on growth areas
- Elaborate 360/page rubrics at 20 people = checkbox culture without behavior change
