## Summary

One slide ("PEOPLE" dimension) from SHIFT Invest's internal "COMPASS" portfolio-support framework — the same impact-VC fund behind the two practitioner interviews (src-087–097, src-112). The framework maps three growth phases — Pioneering (<8 people), Organising (8–25 people), Managing (25–50 people) — against four dimensions (strategy, people, operations, finance); only the PEOPLE dimension was shared. For each phase it states the challenges that define it, what should minimally be in place (team composition, high-performance-culture markers, HR-organization maturity), and the fund's own role and toolbox at that phase.

## Notable claims

- Pioneering: company revolves around the founder(s), little structure or process, limited hierarchy; team needs commercial + execution skills; SHIFT reviews/challenges next hires and org plans, and for senior hires may screen candidates or share profiles.
- Organising: leadership must shift from working harder to smarter; employees expect growth opportunities; more structure is desired; target a complete MT including a senior "closer" with scaling/fundraising experience, a (supervisory) board, and >30% women on the MT; basic HR process, salary house, first talent-retention plans; SHIFT reviews/challenges MT roles, org chart, and HR topics.
- Managing (25–50p): early employees/leaders start underperforming or leaving; confusion over who owns what; open question whether the founding team can scale internationally; target little dependency on any one person, a high-performing supervisory board, an operational blueprint, MT perceived as good leaders by employees, a confidential in-house HR person to go to, clear development/retention/promotion strategy; SHIFT's role is to ensure the supervisory board functions well (independent chair, diversity) and to challenge readiness for the next phase.
- The framework has no phase past 50 people — it does not cover what this wiki calls "Scaled."
