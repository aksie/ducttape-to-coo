## Summary

Molly Graham (Facebook/Quip/Lambda School HR operator) describes a startup performance-review system designed to work with leveling and compensation. Perf cycles 1–2× per year force company-wide consistency on expectations, pay-for-performance, and payroll budget use. Each cycle produces a numerical performance rating and a binary promotion decision; those outputs feed a compensation spreadsheet for salary and equity multipliers. Cycles should be timed to coincide with compensation changes.

## Notable claims

- Proper perf cycles force the company to look at the whole org 1–2× per year for consistent expectations, compensation standards, rewarding high performers, and using payroll budget wisely
- Perf system works hand-in-hand with leveling and compensation; cycle results drive salary raises, title/level growth, and other compensation changes
- Each perf cycle produces two outputs per employee: a numerical performance rating and a binary promotion Yes/No — inputs to formulaic comp
- To tie performance to compensation, both must happen in the same time period; typical schedule is full perf + comp review in January, second perf cycle in June/July with comp changes for promotions and tenure adjustments
- Calibration — managers aligning on what "meets expectations" means at a given level — is essential when perf and comp are linked
