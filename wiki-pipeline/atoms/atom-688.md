## Claim

Don’t wait until the founder “feels ready” to lead — run small experiments where they act as leader (coach, set outcomes, stay out of the fire) until the identity catches up.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Ibarra: identity transitions lag role transitions; change happens through behavioural experiments and new networks (outsight), not by deciding first.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Founders who wait for trust or for the new self-story never start. Practice of leader behaviours is the mechanism that updates identity — same logic as “give away the job every couple of months.”
