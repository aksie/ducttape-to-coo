## Claim

Removing a director the moment margin dips, while they are spending on the team's development, can kill the investment before it returns.

## Source quote or paraphrase

A new director at a weak branch funded learning trips. Margin fell below the norm and he was removed quickly.

## Why (inferred or stated)

A margin floor with no room for a deliberate investment reads every development spend as failure.
