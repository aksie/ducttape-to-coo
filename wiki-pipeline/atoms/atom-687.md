## Claim

Use Immunity-to-Change-style coaching as an evidence-backed option when founders need behaviour change, not only process templates — evidence is promising but still thin (one key quantitative study).

## Source quote or paraphrase

Markus (2016): supervisors receiving ITC coaching reported significantly more progress on leadership development goals than a comparison group.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Gives advisors a non-vendor rationale for recommending coaching. Caveat honesty: one strong study plus coaching metas — pair with structural job-giving, don’t sell coaching as magic.
