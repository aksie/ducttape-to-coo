## Claim

If you never replace yourself with leaders better than you in each major domain, you stay Doer-in-Chief and slow everyone else down.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Rowghani: replace yourself by hiring people better than you into leadership; Weebly's Rusenko learns features from the blog — aspire to a team so good you need not be in product details; without delegating you slow others down.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Hiring peers of yourself who still need your hands on every decision is fake Phase 2. The test is whether day-to-day Phase 1 work runs without you — including product.
