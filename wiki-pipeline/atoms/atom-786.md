## Claim

You hear the hard feedback only after the person has already decided to leave.

## Source quote or paraphrase

McEachran describes a survey loop that returns harsh feedback weeks later, by which point people who were going to quit have gone. He cites a gap of many weeks between the comment and any action.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

A monthly conversation is slow enough. A survey you read next quarter is how you learn about a resignation from a form.
