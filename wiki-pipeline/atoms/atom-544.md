## Claim

Before hiring, calculate your **true monthly burn** — all cash leaving the business each month, not salary alone: payroll loaded, contractors, software, marketing, taxes, insurance, and hidden operational spend.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Under30CEO: burn is total cash out per month; a $70k salary often lands at $85–95k all-in, adding ~$7–8k/month to burn immediately.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Founders who only count salary underestimate burn by 20–40% and overestimate runway — the hire that "fits" on paper doesn't fit in cash.
