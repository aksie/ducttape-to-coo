## Claim

You cancel the conversation when the week gets busy.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Several HN managers say they almost never cancel, and that skipping the slot is how people stop believing it matters. One calls a skipped 1:1 a signal that the person is less important than the fire.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

The conversation only works if it survives a busy week. Cancelling it teaches people to save the real thing for never.
