## Claim

Listen first. Bring advice only when they ask for it.

## Source quote or paraphrase

One manager keeps no agenda of his own, practices listening, and holds back advice unless asked. Another says confidentiality holds except where the law requires you to act.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Advice too early turns their time into your meeting. Listening is how you hear the thing they would not say in the group.
