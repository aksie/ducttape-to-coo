## Claim

In a people business, refuse staff functions. Put functional care of the consultants in the management team, not in a head office.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Staff functions were forbidden. Each management-team member looked after a group of consultants.

## Why (inferred or stated)

Overhead departments recreate the hierarchy the cell model was meant to avoid, and they pull decisions away from the people doing the work.
