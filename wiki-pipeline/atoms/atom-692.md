## Claim

At scale, treat people leaving — and the temporary holes that creates — as a constant operating condition, not an exception; if every departure feels like a crisis, you are still mistaking Whitewater for a rough quarter.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Practitioner: leadership may dismiss Whitewater as a rough quarter; especially at a certain scale, attrition holes are constant, not exceptional.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Informal ownership only works when the same few people never leave. Growth guarantees churn and leave. Documentation and named backups are how you stop paying a full restart cost on every hole.
