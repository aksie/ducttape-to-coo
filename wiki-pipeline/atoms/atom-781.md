## Claim

About once a month, ask what is going well, what is not, which talents you are not using, and the one thing they would change.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Meiert keeps ordinary 1:1s open, and about once a month asks a stay set that includes unused talents and the one change they would make. He traces the talent question to Franklin's stay interviews.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

The talent question is how you hear that a strong person is bored before they leave. The one-change question is the dislike they will not put in a group meeting.
