## Claim

Accountability without authority is the root failure at every stage — if someone is responsible for an outcome, they must be able to decide on it.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Practitioner spine: authority and accountability must sit together; splitting them is why scale-ups stall.

## Why

Teams wait for approval, founders re-enter decisions, and 'the system doesn't work' when the real issue is decision rights not org charts.
