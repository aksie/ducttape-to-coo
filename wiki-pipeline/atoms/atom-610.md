## Claim

Before your first review cycle, define three or four informal performance levels and five behavioral dimensions — what good code, ownership, and collaboration look like at your company.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Hoff: at ~20 people define four informal levels plus specific dimensions (code quality, ownership, collaboration, growth); share with new hires day one.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Without shared levels, reviews become manager opinion and engineers don't know what excellence means until they leave.
