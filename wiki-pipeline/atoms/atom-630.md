## Claim

Expect today's organisational fix to become tomorrow's crisis — the solution of one growth phase sows the seeds of the next revolution.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Greiner: managers experience the irony of seeing a major solution in one period become a major problem in a later period; each phase is both result of the previous phase and cause of the next.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

New practices enable a stretch of evolution, then outlast their usefulness as size and complexity change.
