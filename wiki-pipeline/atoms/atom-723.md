## Claim

Train managers explicitly on how to give feedback — don't assume the skill transfers from running weekly 1-on-1s at a smaller scale.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Raw Signal / First Round: untrained managers know they have feedback to give but don't know how to frame it without damaging the relationship; when given actual skills, adoption is high.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

First-time managers are promoted without feedback skills; at growth you have multiple managers running reviews, so the founder's informal example no longer scales.
