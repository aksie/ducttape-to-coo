## Claim

If you are not excellent at turning finance into operations, hire a partner for that role. Do not try to hire someone to be the story in your place.

## Source quote or paraphrase

The post says hire a partner for the finance-to-operations side if the founder is not world class at it, and that hiring a visionary is very hard.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

The operator job can be filled by a partner. The story is much harder to hand to a stranger, and a weak retelling is how the company loses its point.
