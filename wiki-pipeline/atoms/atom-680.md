## Claim

Your job as CEO is to build the organisation's capacity to handle fires — not to personally put them out.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Sam Corcos (via First Round): build fire departments, not put out fires.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Ops duct tape persists when the founder remains the on-call firefighter. Leadership means systems and owners that extinguish without you; heroics keep maturity at informal ownership.
