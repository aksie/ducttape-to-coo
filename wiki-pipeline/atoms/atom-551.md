## Claim

**Exclude venture debt or bank facilities from core runway** — have the line for peace of mind, but plan spend and runway as if that money does not exist.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Hayes: founders include debt line in runway calculations; pretend the money doesn't exist or you treat it as a rainy-day fund and burn through both.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Counting undrawn facility as runway hides true months-to-zero; when you draw it, burn accelerates through two pools and surprise arrives late.
