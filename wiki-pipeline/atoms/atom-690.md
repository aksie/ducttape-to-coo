## Claim

Treat workplace coaching as a validated support for goal-directed self-regulation and performance when founders must change how they lead — not as a substitute for renaming owners.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Theeboom et al. meta-analysis: coaching shows positive effects across performance/skills, well-being, coping, attitudes, and goal-directed self-regulation (~18 studies, ~2,500 people).

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Gives the intervention mix evidence: coaching helps the identity/self-regulation side; decision-rights and job-giving fix the organisational side. Use both.
