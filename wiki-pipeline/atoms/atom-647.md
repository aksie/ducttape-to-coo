## Claim

When you negotiate each funding round, treat board seat-appointment rights — who gets to appoint which seat — as your real governance lever, not dual-class stock.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Ask HN thread: seat-appointment structure is the practical mechanism that determines control, more than super-voting share structures, which are uncommon outside unusually competitive deals.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Investors will negotiate hard over who appoints board seats because that's the lever that actually functions day to day; a founder who focuses only on share-class mechanics is negotiating the wrong point of leverage.
