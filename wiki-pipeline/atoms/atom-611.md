## Claim

Start each cycle with a short self-review: proudest win, hardest surprise, where you want to grow, and what support would help — about 30 minutes.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Hoff self-review prompts: proud of, harder than expected, growth wants, support needed; reflection not resume.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Self-review surfaces what the employee cares about and where they see gaps before the manager talks.
