## Claim

After each weekly 1-on-1, log the feedback you exchanged in the shared doc — that trail is what you prep formal reviews from.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Add the feedback you shared with them (and they shared with you) in the agenda doc so you can refer back and have a trail of feedback.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Quarterly and annual check-ins become summaries of documented conversations; without a log, managers reconstruct from memory and employees experience formal feedback as new.
