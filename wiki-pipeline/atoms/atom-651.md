## Claim

If you're afraid to share bad news with your board, your investors will find out about real problems too late for anyone to actually help.

## Source quote or paraphrase

David Paul: fear-based board dynamics degrade the information a board needs to do its job, which is the opposite of what tightening scrutiny is meant to achieve.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

A board can only act on problems it knows about; if fear delays disclosure, the board's added scrutiny arrives after the window for course-correction has already narrowed, making the scrutiny reflex self-defeating.
