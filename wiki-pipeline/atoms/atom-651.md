## Claim

Map the specific capability areas your business needs to excel in, and deliberately make sure a board member exists who can speak with authority on each one.

## Source quote or paraphrase

NFX: who is actually in the room matters more than almost any other factor in whether board meetings are useful.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

A board that's well-composed for your specific risks can give substantive input on the decisions that actually matter to your business; a board seated purely by who wrote the biggest check can't, regardless of how well the meeting itself is run.
