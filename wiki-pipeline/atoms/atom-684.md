## Claim

When a founder’s self-worth is staked on being the doer who wins, asking them to stop owning primary processes registers as an identity threat — not a process upgrade.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Crocker and Wolfe: self-worth is contingent on success in domains people have staked their identity on; threats trigger defensiveness.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Advisors who frame the ask only as skills (“learn to delegate”) miss the status and self-worth hit. Effective convincing names the identity cost and designs a new domain where worth can land (architect/leader outcomes).
