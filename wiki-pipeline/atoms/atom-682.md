## Claim

Convincing a founder to “let go” is not the same as convincing them never to touch the work — cargo-cult micromanagement and cargo-cult hands-off both fail.

## Source quote or paraphrase

HN on founder mode: false dichotomy between hire-good-people-and-leave-them and constant countermanding; micromanaging because famous founders dive deep is cargo culting.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Advisors who only push “delegate everything” lose founders who still need deep involvement in product/customers. The change ask must be specific: which primary *ops* processes leave informal ownership, not a blanket personality rewrite.
