## Claim

Holacracy and PRINCE2 are cautionary tales — absorb the principles, reject the theatre.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Principles are sound but full systems become process-heavy theatre; ~90% of people want principles in their own style.

## Why

Rule-keeping replaces operating the business; resentment builds when people must perform the system.
