## Claim

Around 25–50 people, explicitly ask whether the founding leadership team is fit to grow the company internationally — don't assume the team and habits that built the company domestically will scale into new markets by default.

## Source quote or paraphrase

The framework lists "is the founding leadership team fit to grow the company internationally" as one of the defining open questions of this phase, alongside confusion over ownership and early leaders starting to underperform or leave.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

The framework doesn't state an explicit mechanism, but groups this question with two other structural-strain signals (role confusion, early-leader attrition) — the implication is that international scaling requires different leadership capabilities than domestic 0-to-25 growth, and that gap is easy to miss because the team that got you this far still looks "successful."
