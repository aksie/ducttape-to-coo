## Claim

At **~7–10 people**, share **cash in bank, monthly burn, and runway** with the team — early believers took risk; hiding the numbers erodes trust and hides problems others could spot.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Multiple HN founders (~7–10 headcount): share cash, burn, runway openly; dopeboy CEO shares everything with early teammates.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Small teams cannot help on cash timing or cost discipline if only founders see the sheet; opacity also drives employees to assume worst-case and disengage.
