## Claim

A formal review cycle (bi-annual or annual) is in place and tied to compensation, with promotion criteria documented — not left as an unwritten judgment call each cycle.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Molly Graham: perf cycles 1–2× per year produce numerical ratings and binary promotion decisions that feed compensation formulas; cycles timed with comp changes force company-wide consistency.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

At growth, informal founder judgment on pay and promotion doesn't scale across multiple managers and becomes a retention and fairness problem.
