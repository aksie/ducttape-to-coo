## Claim

When scale-up friction appears, do not double down on the start-up strengths that used to work — those same strengths become the kryptonite of the next stage.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Turner Bob case: visionary who hammers every problem, grabs underperformed tasks, cancels management meetings; investors and team needed plan and delegation; he concluded the team should just get on with it. Strengths that raised capital and attracted talent became scale-up kryptonite when he doubled down.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Founders often assume who they are is how they lead; when the stage changes, more of the old mode increases damage instead of fixing it.
