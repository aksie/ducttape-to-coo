## Claim

Treat the leadership crisis as a signal that the organisation needs the next management pattern — not as proof the founder failed.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Greiner: the critical task in each revolutionary period is to find a new set of organisational practices for the next evolutionary growth; founders often resist stepping aside even when unsuited to the next job.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

The practices that unlocked the last phase become the problem of the next; resisting the handoff keeps the company stuck in revolution.
