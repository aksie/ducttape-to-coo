## Claim

If the founder sincerely agrees they should stop duct-taping ops and still doesn’t, look for competing commitments — agreement without behaviour change is the immunity pattern.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Kegan and Lahey: people can commit to a change goal and still fail because hidden competing commitments protect them from feared consequences.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Treating non-follow-through as laziness or stupidity wastes the advisory relationship. Diagnose what the old behaviour is protecting (control, status, fear of looking incompetent) before pushing another checklist.
