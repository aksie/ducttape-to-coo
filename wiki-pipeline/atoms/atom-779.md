## Claim

Do not store this week's friction until the monthly lunch. Say it this week.

## Source quote or paraphrase

A manager who uses a monthly tea for morale and happiness also says they do not wait for the 1:1 to discuss things that should be said sooner, especially feedback.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

A month is the right gap for 'how are you, really.' It is the wrong gap for a problem that is already costing them the week.
