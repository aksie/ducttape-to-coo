## Claim

After you install directive management, expect an autonomy crisis: people closer to the work need decision rights the centre will not give up.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Greiner Phase 2: lower-level employees feel restricted by centralised hierarchy yet know markets and machinery better than top leaders; the second revolution is a crisis of autonomy; companies that cling to centralised methods lose disenchanted people.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Directive systems that once channelled energy efficiently become too tight for a more diverse organisation; the next growth pattern requires delegation.
