## Claim

A healthy organisation covers four functions — Producer, Administrator, Entrepreneur, Integrator — across the team; no single person can excel at all four.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Adizes PAEI: Produce (short-run effective), Administer (short-run efficient / systematise), Entrepreneur (long-run effective / vision and risk), Integrate (long-run efficient / culture so no one is indispensable). Roles are necessary and together sufficient; any missing role yields a predictable mismanagement pattern.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Short- and long-run effectiveness and efficiency require incompatible focuses; one person cannot hold all four at excellence.
