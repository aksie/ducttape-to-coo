## Claim

Hold a lean ~90-minute calibration with managers so "meets" and "exceeds" mean the same thing across teams.

## Source quote or paraphrase

OpenOrg: calibration normalises standards; for startups a lean 90-minute session can suffice.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

At ~15–25 people with multiple managers, inconsistent standards feel unfair and undermine trust in reviews.
