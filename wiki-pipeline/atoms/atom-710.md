## Claim

Ask your employees directly, not just your management team, whether they experience the leadership as genuinely good leaders — self-assessment from the top is not a reliable substitute for that signal.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Derived from the framework listing "MT perceived as good leaders by employees" as a distinct minimum bar for this phase, separate from the management team's own view of itself.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

If only the leadership team's own view counts, the measure is circular — the entire value of naming this as a target is that it's an outside check the leadership can't grade itself on.
