## Claim

At the end of the creativity phase you hit a leadership crisis: informal, founder-held ways of working can no longer carry a larger company.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Greiner Phase 1: founders are product- and market-oriented and often disdain management; as headcount and production grow they become burdened with unwanted management responsibilities, long for the good old days, and a crisis of leadership begins.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Informal communication, founder energy, and marketplace-reactive decisions do not scale once more people, capital, and controls are required.
