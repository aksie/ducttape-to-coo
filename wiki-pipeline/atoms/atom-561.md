## Claim

Work devices use screen lock (about five minutes), and disk encryption (FileVault, BitLocker, or equivalent) is enabled.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Device lock + auto screen lock + disk encryption.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Screen lock stops casual access; encryption protects data if the device is lost — in NL/EU an unencrypted lost device with personal data is often a reportable incident.
