## Claim

If you already know how people are, keep it monthly or even quarterly. If the answers stay vague, meet more often.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Lew says scale the frequency to what you are learning. Vague or avoidant answers mean meet more often. A clear picture means quarterly or twice a year is enough, even though monthly is common.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Monthly is a starting rhythm, not a rule. The conversation is failing when it becomes polite and empty, and that is when you shorten the gap.
