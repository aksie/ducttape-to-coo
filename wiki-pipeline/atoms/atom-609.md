## Claim

Use Situation–Behaviour–Impact (SBI) scripts for constructive feedback — specific moment, observable action, real consequence.

## Source quote or paraphrase

OpenOrg SBI: "During Tuesday's planning… you interrupted Sarah… impact was the team went quiet."

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

SBI strips emotional labels and turns awkward feedback into factual cause-and-effect managers can repeat in 1-on-1s and reviews.
