## Claim

As you cross roughly 25 people, expect some of your earliest employees or leaders to start underperforming or leaving — treat it as a structural signature of this transition, not automatically a sign that those specific people got worse.

## Source quote or paraphrase

The framework names "early employees/leaders start underperforming and/or leaving" as one of the challenges that defines the 25–50-person phase.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Framed this way in the source without an explicit mechanism, but it lines up with a pattern already elsewhere in the wiki: role requirements shift faster than headcount grows, so people who were the right fit for a 10-person "everybody does everything" team are not automatically the right fit for a 30-person team with real structure — the skill gap is structural, not necessarily personal.
