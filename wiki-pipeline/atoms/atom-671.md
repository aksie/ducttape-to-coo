## Claim

When founders and directors don't share an accurate picture of what the board is for, the failure mode isn't just a wasted meeting — in the worst cases the board actively creates problems for the company.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Kjartan Rist: without this shared understanding, boards fail to add value and, in extreme cases, actually cause more problems than they solve.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

A board that believes its job is oversight-through-reporting will default to scrutiny and second-guessing rather than help; that posture is actively counterproductive precisely in the moments (underperformance, uncertainty) when the company needs the board's judgment most.
