## Claim

Founders stay stuck in the mode that already works because exploitation of known competence systematically crowds out exploration of the next leadership behaviours.

## Source quote or paraphrase

March: returns to exploitation are clearer and nearer; competence traps reduce willingness to learn new practices even when adaptation requires them.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Asking a founder to “just decide to delegate” fights a structural learning bias. Interventions must create protected space for exploration (experiments, temporary role redesign), not only more insight about the old mode’s limits.
