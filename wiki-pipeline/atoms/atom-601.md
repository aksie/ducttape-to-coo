## Claim

Every weekly 1-on-1 uses a shared standing agenda — reports add items, and a fixed **Feedback** slot covers both directions.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Regular agenda every week; direct reports add points; standing agenda item Feedback (them→you and you→them).

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Repeating structure stops important topics (especially feedback) from depending on whoever remembers to raise them; bidirectional slot normalizes upward and downward feedback before review season.
