## Claim

Ask about their energy, whether the direction is clear, and what they would change about how you are leading.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Lew suggests using a skip-level to gauge morale: energy, clarity of direction, the working dynamic, and what the person would improve about the leaders.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Energy and clarity are the engagement questions in plain words. The last one is the feedback they will not give you in the team meeting.
