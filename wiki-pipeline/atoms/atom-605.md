## Claim

Run quarterly check-ins as three steps: employee self-reflection on wins/challenges/next-quarter focus, manager prep from 1-on-1 notes, then a ~45-minute forward-looking conversation.

## Source quote or paraphrase

OpenOrg quarterly check-in: employee short form; manager reviews self-reflection plus 1-on-1 notes; 45-minute discussion on growth and priorities.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Separating prep from conversation lets both sides reflect; manager prep from existing notes keeps the meeting a summary not an ambush.
