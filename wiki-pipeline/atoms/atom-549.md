## Claim

Right after a funding round closes, build an **18-month cash management plan** — before the celebration fades and before you defer thinking about spend.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Rob Hayes / First Round: last thing you want after close is to defer charting spend over 18 months; do it immediately while assumptions are fresh.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Post-raise optimism drives spend faster than plan; without a written horizon, founders discover runway problems months later with less room to correct.
