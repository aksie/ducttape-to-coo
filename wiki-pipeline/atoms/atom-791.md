## Claim

When you can no longer have this conversation with everyone yourself, the monthly walk stops being the system.

## Source quote or paraphrase

HN managers describe the load at about thirteen people, and another thread says that once you cannot interact with everyone, intermediaries and 1:1s take over. The wiki already has a weekly written 1:1 at early revenue.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Early revenue is where the founder walk becomes each manager's more frequent one-to-one, with a note. A survey starts to mean something only when a comment is no longer a name.
