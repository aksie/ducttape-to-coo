## Claim

Organisational growth is not smooth: periods of steady evolution under one management pattern end in revolutions that force a new pattern.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Greiner: each phase begins with evolution (steady growth and stability) and ends with a revolutionary period of organisational turmoil; resolution of each revolution determines whether the company moves into the next evolutionary stage.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Past practices that fit a smaller size and earlier time stop working; clinging to them stalls growth or causes failure.
