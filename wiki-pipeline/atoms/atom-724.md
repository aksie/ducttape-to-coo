## Claim

Managers avoid hard conversations, letting performance issues fester — especially once review cycles are formal but managers aren't trained to run them.

## Source quote or paraphrase

First Round / Raw Signal: people know they need to give regular feedback but it often doesn't go well; untrained managers don't know how to frame critical feedback without blowing up the relationship.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Formal cycles without feedback skill create a gap between process and behaviour — issues surface only at review time (see also src-077 / atom-594).
