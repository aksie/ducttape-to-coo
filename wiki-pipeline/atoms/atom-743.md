## Claim

On a founder board, the default is that everyone agrees. A majority vote exists, and one person can still pause a decision when they feel it is wrong and cannot yet explain why.

## Source quote or paraphrase

In principle all three agreed. If one person felt strongly that something was wrong, even without the words yet, it was not pushed through.

## Why (inferred or stated)

The pause is for a felt objection that needs time to become an argument, not a standing veto.
