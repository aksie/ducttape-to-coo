## Claim

Your cash plan assumes **zero revenue** — you never rely on projected inflows to justify spend you cannot afford without them.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Hayes: wants cash plan assuming absolutely zero revenue because you never know; projected revenue in the plan is a common founder mistake.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Revenue timing slips constantly at first hires; spending as if it arrives on schedule burns cash you do not have and narrows options when sales miss.
