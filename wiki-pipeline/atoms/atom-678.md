## Claim

If you delegate urgent-but-unimportant work, you burn relationship capital on interruptions about tasks you do not actually care about.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Do not delegate tasks that are urgent yet unimportant to you — you trade social capital for a stressed person chasing you about work you do not value.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Bad first delegations teach the founder that “delegation doesn’t work,” which reinforces holding primary processes. Start with important, autonomous domains instead.
