## Claim

For every hiring decision, run a simple **runway before / after** table: cash on hand, monthly burn, runway in months — before and after the hire's fully loaded cost.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Under30CEO worked example: $200k cash, $12k burn → 16.6 months; after hire $19k burn → 10.5 months — six months of survival removed by one person.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Making the month-count visible turns "we need this person" into "this person costs us six months" — the trade-off founders must see before signing.
