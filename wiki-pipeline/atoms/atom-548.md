## Claim

Payroll is the most dangerous early-stage expense because it **compounds every month** — founders rarely fail from one bad month; they fail from locked-in recurring burn that shortens runway faster than expected.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Under30CEO cites Paul Graham's rule: payroll compounds monthly; failures come from expenses that shorten runway unexpectedly.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

One-off spend ends; payroll repeats until you cut headcount — so hire mistakes and burn underestimation are multi-month, not one-time, hits to survival.
