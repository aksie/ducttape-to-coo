## Claim

Keep the unpleasant people decisions with the manager: no raise, a hard review. Do not push those onto employees.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Leadership meant giving clarity so professionals could focus. Giving no raise and delivering a bad review was management's work.

## Why (inferred or stated)

Peers will avoid the hard call or turn it into a popularity vote.
