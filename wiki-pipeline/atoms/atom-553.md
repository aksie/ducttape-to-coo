## Claim

A **bad hire at ~12 months runway** can cost **2–3 months** of founder distraction before you cut — enough to jeopardize the whole company.

## Source quote or paraphrase

HN comment on hiring anti-patterns: 12 months runway, burning 2–3 months on a Smart Jerk who isn't a fit — might jeopardize the whole company.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Runway is time; management attention on a failed hire is time not spent on product, sales, or fundraising — at short runway both burn in parallel.
