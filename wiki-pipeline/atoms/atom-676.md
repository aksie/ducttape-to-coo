## Claim

Make yourself redundant on today's job every day — then hire yourself tomorrow for the next higher-leverage job.

## Source quote or paraphrase

HN commenter: fire yourself daily because you made yourself redundant; hire yourself the next day to do something else.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Delegation sticks only if the founder treats role exit as a continuous practice, not a one-time handoff. Without deliberate redundancy, every new hire still routes through the founder's old job.
