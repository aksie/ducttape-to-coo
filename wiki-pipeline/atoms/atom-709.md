## Claim

By this stage, your organization runs according to a documented operational blueprint rather than tribal knowledge held by a few people, and your management team is perceived by employees — not just by itself — as genuinely good leaders.

## Source quote or paraphrase

The framework's "what should minimally be in place" for this phase lists "organization working according to operational blueprint" and "MT perceived as good leaders by employees" as high-performance-culture markers.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Two separate failure modes get conflated without this: a management team can believe it's leading well while actually operating on undocumented tribal knowledge that only works because the same few people are still around. Naming "perceived as good leaders by employees" as a distinct target forces a check against the outside view, not just the leadership team's own self-assessment.
