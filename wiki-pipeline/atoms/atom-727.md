## Claim

Document promotion criteria as a rubric — assess readiness for the next level against explicit criteria, not ad hoc manager judgment.

## Source quote or paraphrase

AltSchool / Ventilla: promotion packets show % completion of next-level criteria (e.g. Level-4 employee at 100%/80%/60%/20% on Level-5 dimensions); without formal levels and performance data, comp and advancement decisions are arbitrary.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Star performers hired over without prior feedback on management skills is a common failure when promotion criteria stay implicit until a reorg.
