## Claim

Don't wait until Series A or B to take board-meeting discipline seriously — the communication and relationship-building skill compounds, so starting early is worth more than starting formally.

## Source quote or paraphrase

NFX: board meetings done right are a management and communication skill that, learned early, compounds in value for the company.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Skills built under lower stakes (informal early meetings) transfer to higher-stakes formal board meetings later, so treating board discipline as something to defer until it's legally required wastes the years where it was cheapest to build.
