## Claim

Ask how burned out they feel, as a number, instead of waiting for them to say it.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Meiert added a 0–10 burnout question after finding that people do not volunteer overload. He kept it through later revisions because it caught load he would otherwise have missed.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

At this size one overloaded person is the team. A number is easier to answer than a confession.
