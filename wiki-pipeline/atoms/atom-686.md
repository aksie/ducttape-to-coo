## Claim

Map the founder’s competing commitments and big assumptions about letting go before you push another delegation plan.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Immunity to Change: surface competing commitments and the big assumptions underneath so adaptive change becomes coachable.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Structural moves (name an owner, give away a job) stick only if the fear those moves trigger is handled. The map turns vague resistance into a specific assumption you can test with small experiments.
