## Claim

You install a formal round while everyone still talks all day, and the round becomes ceremony.

## Source quote or paraphrase

An HN commenter argues that under about fifty people everyone already talks to everyone, so a special meeting to extract what is wrong is unnecessary. Others answer that a scheduled slot is how you hear what people will not say in the open.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

The test is whether a worry still reaches you without an appointment. If it does, keep the monthly walk short. If it does not, the walk is replacing the hallway.
