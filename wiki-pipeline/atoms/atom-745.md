## Claim

A board that aligns on offsites but never gives its own members hard feedback will let things simmer, and the people below will notice the double standard.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Offsites once or twice a year aligned the big topics. Board members did not coach each other. Branch directors, who were assessed, saw the inequality.

## Why (inferred or stated)

The feedback system is not credible if the people who impose it are exempt.
