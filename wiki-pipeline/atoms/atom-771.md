## Claim

Results were treated as the outcome of hiring, training and selling well, not as the thing to manage directly.

## Source quote or paraphrase

The company paid little attention to the financial result itself, on the bet that good people and good processes would produce it.

## Why (inferred or stated)

The bet held except when scale created liquidity squeezes. The exception is the cash process, not a reason to manage only the result.
