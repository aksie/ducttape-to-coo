## Claim

As the company scales, you flex between three leadership modes — Brave Warrior, Considered Architect, and Wise Monarch — rather than leading from one default style.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Turner WAM model: Brave Warrior for start-up (fast, single-minded, risk-comfortable); Considered Architect for scale-up (systems thinker, planner, systematiser); Wise Monarch for grown-up company (strategist, diplomat, figurehead). Modes are right or wrong for a challenge, not absolutely.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

What the team, customers, and investors need from the founder changes radically by stage; one natural style cannot cover the whole journey.
