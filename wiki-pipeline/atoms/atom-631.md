## Claim

Your board's legal power to remove you exists in essentially every VC-backed company, not only in ones with unusually founder-unfriendly terms — don't assume your deal was different.

## Source quote or paraphrase

HN thread: the "board works for the founder" framing understates the board's actual authority once institutional investors hold seats, and this is the default, not the exception.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Standard VC board seats and standard fiduciary duty rules give directors real removal authority by default; a founder needs to negotiate specifically to change that, not assume it away.
