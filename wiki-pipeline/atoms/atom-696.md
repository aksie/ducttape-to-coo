## Claim

Protect CEO time for three non-delegable jobs: hire and coach the senior team so they work well together; write and over-communicate Mission–Strategy–Metrics; co-author authentic culture with early people.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Rowghani: only the CEO can hire/ensure senior leaders work together; create purpose and alignment (Mission, Strategy, Metrics); nurture culture — everyone's job to live, not CEO solo authorship of values.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Gives founders a positive job description for "what I do after I stop doing everything" — answers team confusion and identity loss when the doer role ends.
