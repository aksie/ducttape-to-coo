## Claim

A healthy investor relationship applies pressure when you need it and eases off when you don't — it isn't a fixed adversarial stance in either direction.

## Source quote or paraphrase

David Paul: knowing when to push and when to ease off is a discernible skill on the investor side, not a fixed personal style — the same investor is capable of both.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Constant pressure erodes trust and information flow (see the fear mechanism above); constant passivity fails to hold founders accountable — the useful middle state requires the investor to actually read the situation each time rather than defaulting to one mode.
