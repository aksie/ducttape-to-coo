## Claim

Treat the slot as theirs. Do not fill it with project status.

## Source quote or paraphrase

HN managers say the 1:1 is the employee's time. Project status belongs in the standup. The conversation is about their problems, goals, and whether the work still fits them.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Status crowds out the thing you cannot hear in a group: whether they are all right, and whether the work is the work they should be doing.
