## Claim

Early-success founders are typically strong on Entrepreneur and Producer and weak on Administrator — so when growth demands process they experience it as boring, slowing, or bureaucratic and quietly resist it.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Adizes typical path: Go-Go organisations run PaEi (P and E strong; A and I missing). E passionately resists A even while needing it; successive Administrators get destroyed in a Go-Go culture if E will not step back. Pathological form: founder's trap when A and I never develop.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

E and P created the success and the founder's identity; A threatens that identity and tempo, so the missing function is the one the founder is least willing to install.
