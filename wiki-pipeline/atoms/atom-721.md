## Claim

Even before you have a formal board, expect an engaged early investor to review and challenge your next hires and org plans — for senior hires, they may want to screen key candidates or have profiles shared with them.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Framework describes the fund's role at the under-8-person phase as: "review/challenge next hires, org plans. For senior hires, potentially screen key-candidates, or share profiles."
