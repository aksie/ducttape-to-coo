## Claim

You put a weekly sit-down with everyone on the calendar and the week fills up with meetings.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Managers with about ten to thirteen people describe 1:1s as a large share of the week. When offered weekly or every two weeks, most people picked every two weeks. Weekly felt like an encroachment to some.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

At 2–10, a monthly deeper conversation is light enough to keep. A weekly one with everyone is the early-revenue rhythm, and it will eat the week if you start it now.
