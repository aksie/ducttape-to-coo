## Claim

Watch for employees starting to expect real growth opportunities and more structured ways of working while your team is still operating free-format ("everybody works on everything") — that gap is where knowledge stops being shared or transferred internally.

## Source quote or paraphrase

The framework lists "employees expect growth opportunities," "lack of sharing and transferring knowledge internally," and "more structure is desired, away from free format" together as challenges defining the 8–25-person phase.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

No explicit mechanism in source. Plausible reading: informal "ask whoever's around" knowledge-sharing works when everyone overlaps constantly at under ~8 people, but stops working once the team is large enough that people specialize and don't naturally cross paths — roughly where this framework places the phase boundary.
