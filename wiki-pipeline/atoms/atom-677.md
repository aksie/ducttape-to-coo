## Claim

When you keep a decision, write down the criteria you used and let others poke holes — that is how they learn to take the next ones.

## Source quote or paraphrase

If the decision must be yours, expose required information and thought process; let others challenge it; repeat so they build case history.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Founders stay stuck as sole deciders because criteria live only in their heads. Teaching the decision algorithm is the bridge from warrior execution to architect leadership.
