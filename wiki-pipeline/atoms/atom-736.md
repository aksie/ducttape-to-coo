## Claim

Say at entry that there are two careers: an all-round leadership path, and a specialist path that is well paid and does not lead into the management team.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Becoming a deep specialist was a valid, well-paid path. It did not lead into the management team.

## Why (inferred or stated)

Without that sentence, strong specialists either resent the leadership track or get promoted into a job they did not want.
