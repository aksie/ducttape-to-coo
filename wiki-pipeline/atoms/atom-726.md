## Claim

Formally tie compensation reviews to performance-review outcomes, and document promotion criteria so advancement isn't a black box.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Molly Graham: cycle outputs (rating + promotion decision) drive salary and equity multipliers; calibration aligns managers on standards at each level.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Undocumented criteria let bias and inconsistency creep in; top performers leave when pay doesn't reflect contribution.
