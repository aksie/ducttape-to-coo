## Claim

Hiring a COO because you are overloaded — without a mandate for structure and honest pushback — does not fix founder bottleneck behaviour.

## Source quote or paraphrase

HN: COO role is discipline, process, cross-team communication, and challenging the CEO; not every busy startup needs the title.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Title without behaviour change leaves the founder overruling ops or dumping undifferentiated work. Convince the founder to change the decision rights first; then staff the role that holds them.
