## Claim

You send an engagement survey to a team small enough that every answer has a name.

## Source quote or paraphrase

McEachran argues that under about ten people the score is a special case, because the leader is the manager, and that an average hides the person. He also argues a survey question lacks the follow-up a conversation can give.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

At 2–10 people a 'confidential' score is not confidential. You already know who is unhappy if you sit with them. The survey adds a number and loses the reason.
