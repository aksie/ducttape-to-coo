## Claim

Structure the review conversation: specific wins with examples, growth areas framed as opportunity, concrete support you'll provide, then comp/career clarity.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Hoff: lead with specific praise, growth as opportunity not failure, concrete support, then raise or explain why comp is flat.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Order matters — starting with vague positivity then dropping comp news feels like a bait-and-switch; clarity on both growth and pay reduces attrition.
