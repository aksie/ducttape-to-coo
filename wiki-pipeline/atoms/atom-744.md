## Claim

When someone blocks a decision without an argument, ask what the delay buys. A short time to get the argument in order is valid. An open postpone is not.

## Source quote or paraphrase

A repeated postpone, compared with a technical leader delaying a pricing call, had to justify itself. After about a week, trust each other's expertise.

## Why (inferred or stated)

Without that question, a block with no content becomes the decision.
