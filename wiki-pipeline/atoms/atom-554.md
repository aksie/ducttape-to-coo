## Claim

When runway is short, a **wrong hire is often worse than no hire** — you pay loaded cost plus leadership time while the role stays empty or damaging.

## Source quote or paraphrase

HN: hiring someone who isn't a good fit often worse than not hiring at startup scale; comfort cutting fast at 2–3 months.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

No hire preserves cash and focus; bad hire consumes both — at 2–10 people you rarely have spare capacity to absorb the mistake.
