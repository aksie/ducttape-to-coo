## Claim

A practical success test for the Phase 2 transition: you have enough unscheduled time to think and stay ahead of the company — not a calendar full of other people's work.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Rowghani citing Jobs: at best ~50% of time unscheduled for thinking, drop-ins, curiosity; without free time you lead from behind; unscheduled time signals leadership team + Mission-to-Metrics are working.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Gives advisors and founders a measurable "are we convincing change?" signal beyond headcount: if the CEO calendar is still all doing, Phase 2 has not landed.
