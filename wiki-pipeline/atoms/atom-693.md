## Claim

Around 20–25 people, shift from Doer-in-Chief to Company-Builder-in-Chief — Phase 1 success (demand outrunning the team) is the trigger to stop doing everything yourself.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Rowghani: Phase 1 CEO builds product as Doer-in-Chief; Phase 2 begins ~20–25 employees when company-building becomes the primary job; ends ~400–500 with a road-tested leadership team.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Without an explicit phase shift, founders keep the behaviour that won Phase 1 and become the bottleneck. Naming the trigger (demand > team capacity / ~20–25 headcount) makes the ask to change concrete.
