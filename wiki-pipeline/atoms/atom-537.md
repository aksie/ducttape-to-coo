## Claim

At first hires you keep a **Cash tab** in the same sheet as your plan: opening balance, monthly burn, runway in months — aligned with cumulative cash on the main P&L tab so the numbers cannot drift apart.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Contribution § Build the base plan — Cash tab separate from People tab; keep aligned with cumulative cash on main tab.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

If cash runway lives in a different place than the plan, founders argue from stale numbers while the sheet shows a different story — especially once payroll lands on the main tab.
