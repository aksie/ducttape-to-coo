## Claim

Formal review feedback that was never logged in weekly 1-on-1 notes feels like rewriting history — people dispute whether it was ever raised.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Without a written trail, review-time feedback becomes a debate about whether something was said months ago.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Memory-based performance conversations undermine trust and recreate the "surprise review" failure mode even when managers believe they gave feedback informally.
