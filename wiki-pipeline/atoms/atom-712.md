## Claim

Push your board toward a genuinely well-functioning supervisory structure at this stage: an independent chair, real diversity among members, and people who will actually challenge your thinking — not just approve decisions you've already made.

## Source quote or paraphrase

The framework describes the fund's own role at this phase as making sure the supervisory board is "well functioning (indep. chair, diversity etc.)."

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Framed from the investor's side in the source, as something they check for. Flipped to the founder's side here — a founder who wants this can ask for it explicitly rather than waiting for an investor to impose it.
