## Claim

Satisfaction at this size sits with the person who manages them, not with a form.

## Source quote or paraphrase

McEachran says the manager, not HR, owns whether people thrive, and that a survey which routes feedback through a slow loop is a cover for not talking. He sells software for the conversation, so the anti-survey conclusion serves the product.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

You do not have an HR loop yet. The walk is the loop. A form would pretend there is a department between you and the answer.
