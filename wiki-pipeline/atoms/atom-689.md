## Claim

Under stress, founders double down on the strength that won early — that overuse is why “do more of what you’re good at” becomes kryptonite at the hard middle.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Kaiser and Kaplan: strengths overused become weaknesses; leaders amplify preferred styles under pressure instead of flexing.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Empirically grounds Turner’s default-mode claim. Convincing change means building versatility (dial down doer mode, dial up architect/admin), not adding more intensity to the old strength.
