## Claim

Update the cash tab **monthly** at minimum; move to a **weekly** founder cash check when runway is tight or cash is lumpy (payroll, tax, large invoices).

## Source quote or paraphrase

Contribution: update monthly; weekly if cash is tight. Cross-ref 2.1 vs 2.2 monthly founder review.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Monthly cadence is enough when runway is comfortable; when cash is tight, payroll and surprises can burn through a month of buffer before the next review — weekly closes that gap without building treasury ops.
