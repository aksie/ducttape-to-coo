## Claim

Even with a structured external assessment, whether a founder or a candidate hire is actually coachable is genuinely hard to predict in advance — build in a real assessment process and a defined point where you act on the answer, rather than trusting your own read at the table.

## Source quote or paraphrase

Partner, asked directly how they assess whether a founder can make the needed personal shift, says it's genuinely very hard to predict. Works with external parties for team assessments specifically because it's hard to do well internally, and describes results as mixed even then — no silver bullet found yet.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

An honest epistemic-humility counterweight to the fund's own confident-sounding capability-mapping and hiring frameworks elsewhere in this research: the same practitioner who built a formal internal maturity framework openly admits coachability prediction resists systematization. Useful as a check against overclaiming certainty in wiki guidance — the actionable takeaway is process (external, structured assessment; a real decision point) rather than confidence in judgment at the door.
