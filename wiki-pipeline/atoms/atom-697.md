## Claim

Manage senior executives to quarterly and annual outputs — not weekly milestones — and fire underperforming leadership hires faster than feels comfortable (expect ~25% not to work out).

## Source quote or paraphrase

Rowghani: weekly milestones fit Phase 1 PMF; executives need longer-term outputs; at least 25% of leadership hires fail; most CEOs take too long to fire their first executive and lose credibility by waiting.

## Why (inferred from source if not explicit)

Founders who "delegate" then micromanage weekly recreates Doer-in-Chief one layer up. Slow firing keeps the org stuck with a bad Phase 2 design.
