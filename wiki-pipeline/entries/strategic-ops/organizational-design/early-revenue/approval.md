- Edited claim text: - **According to YC: A CEO's first job is to build a product users love; the second job is to build a company to maximize the opportunity that the product has surfaced; and the third is to harvest the profits of the core business to invest in transformative new product ideas. Around 20–25 people, Phase 2 company-building becomes the primary CEO job** (through roughly hundreds of people until a road-tested leadership team can run Phase 1 work without you). Phase 1 success — demand outrunning the small team — is the trigger. Without an explicit shift, the behaviour that won Phase 1 becomes the bottleneck.
