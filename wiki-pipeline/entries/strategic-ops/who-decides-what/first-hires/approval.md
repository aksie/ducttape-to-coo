- Edited claim text: - **At Early Revenue, name a decision owner per area and add escalation thresholds.** Founder gives input in meetings; the area owner decides — decisions should not re-open because a founder wasn't in the room. SPADE memos and formal RACI wait until growth/scaled or repeated cross-team conflict at ~15–25 people.

A SPADE memo is a structured decision-making document. It uses the SPADE framework (Setting, People, Alternatives, Decide, Explain) created at Square to solve hard problems. Like a RACI matrix or other decision matrices, it assigns clear ownership so teams do not get stuck on who makes the final call.The SPADE Framework PartsSetting: The background and context of why the choice matters.People: Who leads the choice, who gives input, and who holds veto power.Alternatives: A list of different, realistic paths or solutions.Decide: The step where input is gathered (often via private votes) and the final path is chosen.Explain: The written reason for the final choice shared with the wider group.
