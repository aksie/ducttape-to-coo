- Edited claim text: - **Reversible (Type 1) and non reversible (Type 2) decisions are explicitly described mini processes** — reversible (Type 1) decisions delegate to the role-owner within their mandate; irreversible, cross-cutting bets (Type 2) use an integrative process where a role-owner can block only with a **reasoned objection** (e.g. CFO: action makes solvency impossible), not "I don't like it." Absorb Holacracy's objection principle without the full-system theatre.
