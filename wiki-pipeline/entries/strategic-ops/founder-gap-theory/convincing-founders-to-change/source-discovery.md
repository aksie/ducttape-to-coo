# Source discovery — Convincing founders to change

> **Superseded 2026-09-16.** Role-exit claims → **1.5**; decision-rights thin addendum → **1.4**; board-practice claims (c-019–c-024) → **1.2b** (published with shareholder-reporting batch). Keep this cell as the research trail; do not publish a third wiki page from it.

**Research question (user, Sep 2026):** How can you convince founders to change — to start delegating the primary process, start being a leader, and fix Ops duct tape?

**Why this cell exists:** The published essay *What Got You Here Is What’s Hurting You Now* diagnoses the gap (org needs X; founder still does Y). The March [research annex](../blog/research-annex.md) held an **identity / change layer** for a follow-up. This pipeline run is that follow-up: not “what is the transition,” but **what actually moves a founder through it**.

**Stage-fit lens:** Primary stage = **early-revenue / Whitewater** (~10–50). Actions also apply at late first-hires when the founder is already the bottleneck. Deprioritise enterprise change-management frameworks; prefer founder-specific, operator, and behavioural sources.

## Working synthesis (for Phase 2 — not claims yet)

1. **Hiring ops without founder change fails** — CoS/COO becomes a second bottleneck or gets overruled (HN COO thread + Altman letting-go).
2. **Intervention is job-giving, not pep talks** — “give away your job every couple of months”; fire yourself daily; name owners (links to 1.4).
3. **Identity is the real blocker** — PAEI/Turner already registered; Ibarra + Immunity to Change explain *why agreeing isn’t changing*.
4. **Temporary hands-on ≠ permanent Founder Mode** — high-touch to fix, then step back (HN 34834075); post-~50, bypassing layers fails (HN 41781163).
5. **Advisor move:** diagnose processes still at informal ownership *and* name the identity cost of letting them go — same thesis as the blog, now as intervention design.

### Intervention synthesis (identity + practitioner, for Phase 2)

1. **Competence trap + strengths overuse** explain *why* founders intensify the old mode under stress (March, Kaiser/Kaplan ↔ Turner).
2. **Self-worth contingencies** explain defensiveness when primary processes leave their hands (Crocker ↔ PAEI E+P identity).
3. **Immunity to Change** is the diagnostic for “agrees but doesn’t change”; map competing commitments before another ops checklist (Kegan/Lahey; Markus for efficacy caveat).
4. **Ibarra experiments + Molly Graham job-giving + Rowghani Phase 2** are the behavioural prescription: act as leader / give away the Doer job / protect CEO-only work before identity feels ready.
5. **Coaching metas** justify coaching as support for self-regulation — always paired with naming owners and giving away jobs (HN + 1.4 + YC), never as substitute.
6. **Rowghani success test:** unscheduled think time ≈ Phase 2 landed; calendar full of doing ≈ still Phase 1.
