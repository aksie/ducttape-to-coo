# Learning & Development — Growth

> In a people business the academy is how new hires meet the company. The first months are half the craft and half working with people, and the leaders teach it.

## What good looks like at this phase

<!-- claim-id: c-001 -->
- **Every new hire spends the first months in an in-house academy, about half on the craft and half on working with people.** Here that was the first two months. The second half is the job, not a soft extra.
  <!-- sources: src-117 (commercial-operator, people_business) -->

## What you actually need to do

<!-- claim-id: c-002 -->
1. **Leaders teach most of it.** That keeps them close to new people and shows who is growing. Buy outside teaching only for depth you do not have.
