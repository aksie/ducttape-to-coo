# Employee Satisfaction & Feedback — Growth

> Once cells exist, a company-wide mood is the wrong unit. Pick an instrument that matches the culture you say you have, publish the score per cell, and give it a public ambition.

## What good looks like at this phase

<!-- claim-id: c-003 -->
- **The score has a visible ambition, and pride is treated as part of the job.** Here the ambition was a national top three, every time. They also presented the company as larger than it was, and celebrated in public. The parties were the proof people could describe, not a perk list.
  <!-- sources: src-117 (commercial-operator, people_business) -->

## What you actually need to do

<!-- claim-id: c-001 -->
1. **Pick the survey that matches the philosophy you already stated.** They chose a pride-oriented instrument after deciding work happiness was the point. A survey that measures a different company becomes a second strategy. A sector-specific variant is mentioned in the transcript and is not clear enough to copy.
   <!-- sources: src-117 (commercial-operator, people_business) -->

<!-- claim-id: c-002 -->
2. **Report the score per cell and use it as the conversation with that cell.** The results here were predictable. They still gave each branch a basis for the talk. A company-wide number hides the cell that is sliding.
   <!-- sources: src-117 (commercial-operator, people_business) -->
