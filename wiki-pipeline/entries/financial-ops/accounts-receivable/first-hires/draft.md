# Accounts Receivable (Outgoing Invoices) — First Hires

> At first paying customers, AR is not a system — it is invoice → track → chase. Cash stuck for admin reasons is still cash you do not have.

## What good looks like at this phase

<!-- claim-id: c-001 -->
- **You invoice as soon as the deal is signed** — not when you "get around to admin."
  <!-- sources: src-071 (processes.json stageFocus seed) -->

<!-- claim-id: c-002 -->
- **Payment status lives in one spreadsheet** (or your accountant's tool) you actually look at.
  <!-- sources: src-071 -->

<!-- claim-id: c-003 -->
- **Late payers get a personal follow-up** — chasing is normal at this stage, not a failure.
  <!-- sources: src-071 -->

<!-- claim-id: c-004 -->
- **You know a finance / AP contact at each customer** so invoices do not die in a generic inbox.
  <!-- sources: src-071 -->

## What you actually need to do

<!-- claim-id: c-005 -->
1. **Send the invoice the day the deal closes** (or on the agreed milestone) — same owner who closed it, or a named handoff.
   <!-- sources: src-071 -->

<!-- claim-id: c-006 -->
2. **Log every open invoice:** customer, amount, due date, status, last chase date.
   <!-- sources: src-071 -->

<!-- claim-id: c-007 -->
3. **When something is overdue, message or call the AP contact** — then note the date in the sheet.
   <!-- sources: src-071 -->

<!-- claim-id: c-008 -->
4. **Ask for the right AP / finance ops name at kickoff** so the first invoice is not a cold send into accounts@
   <!-- sources: src-071 -->

## Warning signs you're behind

### Output quality

<!-- claim-id: c-009 -->
- **Deals are closed but unbilled for weeks** — revenue is fictional until the invoice goes out.
  <!-- sources: src-071 -->

### Founder time

<!-- claim-id: c-010 -->
- **Nobody owns chasing** — overdue balances sit until cash is already tight.
  <!-- sources: src-071 -->

### Process entry

<!-- claim-id: c-011 -->
- **You cannot say which invoices are open or who you last chased** without digging through email.
  <!-- sources: src-071 -->

## How this evolves next

At early revenue: standardised templates, clear payment terms, automated reminders, watch average days to collect weekly.

## Tools & resources

- Spreadsheet or bookkeeper invoicing tool — one open-invoice list.
